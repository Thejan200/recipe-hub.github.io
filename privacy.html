Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who operates BiteSparks

BiteSparks is an independent online recipe publication operated by M.G.Y. Thejan Mahakumbura, based in Sri Lanka. For privacy questions, contact thejanmahakumbura@gmail.com.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use browser local storage for optional features such as saved recipes, theme preferences, remembering a cookie-notice choice, and a temporary recipe-rating session. Saved recipes are stored in your browser and are not kept in a BiteSparks account database.

Recipe ratings

If you choose to rate a recipe, BiteSparks uses a temporary anonymous browser session to help limit duplicate ratings. The rating value, recipe identifier, and technical session data are processed by Supabase, our ratings data provider. BiteSparks does not ask for your name or email address to submit a rating.

Before a new anonymous rating session is created, Cloudflare Turnstile runs a security check to help prevent automated or fraudulent ratings. Cloudflare and Supabase may process limited technical information needed to provide those services under their own privacy terms.

Email contact

If you email BiteSparks, the information you provide is used to read and respond to your message, including correction requests or other support enquiries.

Analytics

With your consent, BiteSparks uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregated website use, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, and interaction data. Google Analytics is not loaded until you choose Accept in the cookie and analytics notice. Declining does not affect your ability to use the site.

Google may process analytics information under its own privacy policy. BiteSparks does not use Google Analytics to collect names, email addresses, recipe ratings, or other directly identifying information.

Advertising

BiteSparks displays third-party advertising through Monetag’s In-Page Push format. Advertising supports the operation of this free recipe publication and does not determine which recipes, ratings, or editorial guidance we publish.

Monetag and its advertising partners may process technical information such as the page URL, referrer URL, IP address, country or geographical area, browser, operating system, visit and interaction information to deliver, measure, limit the frequency of, protect against fraud in, and improve advertising. See Monetag’s Privacy Policy, Cookies Policy, and Opt-Out page.

Third-party content

Some recipe pages embed YouTube videos or link to third-party sites. Those providers may collect or process information under their own policies when you use their services.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. You can also choose not to use optional features such as saved recipes or recipe ratings. Choices for optional analytics and advertising technologies are available through the site’s cookie and privacy notice.

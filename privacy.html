Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

Recipe Hub is designed to minimize unnecessary personal-data collection. This policy describes the current website behavior and should be updated when new services are connected.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use local browser storage for optional features such as saved recipes and remembering your cookie-consent choice. Saved recipes are stored in your browser rather than in a Recipe Hub account database.

Forms and newsletter

The current static site does not send contact or newsletter submissions to a server until an approved form or email-marketing provider is configured. Do not enter sensitive information into forms. When a provider is connected, this policy will be updated with the provider, data collected, purpose, retention, and user-rights details.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising service should be treated as active until it has been explicitly configured. If Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, advertising networks, or other tracking services are added, the privacy and consent configuration will be updated before activation.

Third-party content

Embedded YouTube videos and external links are operated by third parties. Those providers may process information according to their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings and decline non-essential cookies when the consent notice is shown. Some convenience features, such as saved recipes, may stop working after storage is cleared or disabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact page. This policy should be reviewed for the actual business, jurisdictions, services, and data practices before commercial launch.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who operates BiteSparks

BiteSparks is an independent online recipe publication operated by M.G.Y. Thejan Mahakumbura, based in Sri Lanka. For privacy questions, contact thejanmahakumbura@gmail.com.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use browser local storage for optional features such as saved recipes, theme preferences, and remembering a cookie-notice choice. Saved recipes are stored in your browser and are not kept in a BiteSparks account database.

Email contact

If you email BiteSparks, the information you provide is used to read and respond to your message, including correction requests or other support enquiries.

Advertising and analytics

Google AdSense is not currently active on BiteSparks and Google ads are not currently served by the site. If AdSense, analytics, or other non-essential advertising technology is enabled in the future, this policy and the cookie notice will be updated before activation to describe the relevant data use and choices.

Third-party content

Some recipe pages embed YouTube videos or link to third-party sites. Those providers may collect or process information under their own policies when you use their services.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. You can also choose not to use optional features such as saved recipes. Where non-essential technologies are introduced, the site will provide the applicable choices before they are enabled.

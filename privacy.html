Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

BiteSparks is designed to minimize unnecessary personal-data collection. This policy explains how the website handles information while you browse and use its features.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use local browser storage for optional features such as saved recipes and remembering your cookie-consent choice. Saved recipes are stored in your browser rather than in a BiteSparks account database.

Email contact

When you contact BiteSparks by email, the information you send is used only to read and respond to your message.

Analytics and advertising

BiteSparks does not currently use analytics or advertising tracking services.

Third-party content

Embedded YouTube videos and external links are operated by third parties. Those providers may process information according to their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings and decline non-essential cookies when the consent notice is shown. Some convenience features, such as saved recipes, may stop working after storage is cleared or disabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact page or email the site owner directly at the address listed there.

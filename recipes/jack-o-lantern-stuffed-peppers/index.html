Jack-O-Lantern Stuffed Peppers

Orange bell peppers carved with friendly faces and filled with seasoned rice, beans and cheese.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Do not cut face openings too large or filling can spill. A common setback is Overpacking prevents the rice centre from heating evenly.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 55 min🍽 6 servings🔥 305 cal
DinnerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 6 orange bell peppers
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 cup corn
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon cumin

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and cut lids from the peppers, removing seeds and membranes.
  2. Step 2.Use a small knife to cut simple triangle eyes and a smile into one side of each pepper.
  3. Step 3.Mix rice, beans, corn, salsa, cumin and half the cheese in a bowl.
  4. Step 4.Fill the peppers loosely, top with remaining cheese and replace the lids at a slight angle.
  5. Step 5.Bake until peppers are tender and filling is hot throughout, then serve upright.

Breakfast Tacos

Warm tortillas filled with softly cooked eggs, savoury chorizo, melting cheese, avocado, and bright pico de gallo for a flexible Tex-Mex breakfast.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Keep tortillas covered after warming to prevent their edges from drying out. To avoid a common problem, remember: High heat sets scrambled eggs too quickly and can make them rubbery.

Ingredients

  • 8 small flour or corn tortillas
  • 8 large eggs
  • 12 oz fresh chorizo or bacon
  • 1/4 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or Mexican-blend cheese
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cup pico de gallo
  • Fresh cilantro and hot sauce, for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Set a skillet over medium heat and cook the chorizo, breaking it apart with a spoon, until no pink meat remains and the pieces are browned.
  2. Step 2.Spoon the chorizo onto a warm plate, leaving just enough rendered fat in the pan to flavour the next component if desired.
  3. Step 3.Heat the tortillas one at a time in a dry pan or briefly over a low flame until pliable with a few toasted spots.
  4. Step 4.Wrap the warmed tortillas in a clean towel or keep them covered so they stay flexible.
  5. Step 5.Beat the eggs, milk, and salt together until the yolks and whites are fully combined.
  6. Step 6.Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat, then add the egg mixture.
  7. Step 7.Draw a spatula slowly across the base of the pan as soft curds form; take the pan off the heat while they still look glossy.
  8. Step 8.Place eggs in the centre of each tortilla and scatter the cheese over them while both are warm.
  9. Step 9.Finish with chorizo, avocado, pico de gallo, cilantro, and as much hot sauce as suits the table.
  10. Step 10.Fold or serve open-faced straight away, before the tortillas cool and the eggs firm up.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep tortillas covered after warming to prevent their edges from drying out.
  • Gentle heat gives the scrambled eggs a tender texture and leaves time to assemble the toppings.
  • Put toppings in small bowls so each person can adjust salsa, avocado, and hot sauce.

! Common Mistakes

  • High heat sets scrambled eggs too quickly and can make them rubbery.
  • Filling tortillas straight from the package makes them more likely to tear when folded.
  • Packing in too much filling can cause the taco to split and spill.

↔ Substitutions

  • Crisp bacon is a suitable swap when fresh chorizo is unavailable.
  • Use corn tortillas for a naturally gluten-free option, checking all packaged ingredients as needed.
  • Seasoned black beans can replace the meat for a vegetarian filling.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled eggs and chorizo in separate covered containers for up to 3 days.
  • Warm eggs slowly in a skillet and heat tortillas separately before assembling.
  • Cut avocado and add pico de gallo only at serving time for the freshest texture.

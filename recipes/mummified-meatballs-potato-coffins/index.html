Mummified Meatballs in Potato Skin Coffins

Crisp potato-skin coffins holding saucy meatballs with cheese-strip mummy wraps.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Crisp the potato skins before adding sauce. A common setback is Excess sauce makes the skins collapse.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 45 min🍽 12 servings🔥 175 cal
AppetizerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 6 small baked potatoes
  • 18 cooked meatballs
  • 1 cup marinara
  • 1 cup mozzarella strips
  • olive slices

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Halve baked potatoes and scoop out most of the flesh, leaving a sturdy potato-skin shell.
  2. Step 2.Trim the shells into simple coffin shapes and roast cut-side up until crisp.
  3. Step 3.Warm meatballs in marinara until hot throughout, then place one or two in each potato coffin.
  4. Step 4.Lay mozzarella strips across the meatballs as loose mummy wraps.
  5. Step 5.Broil briefly until cheese melts and finish with olive eyes before serving.

Appetizer · Easy

Fairy Bread

A cheerful Australian party snack of buttered white bread covered in colourful sprinkles.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 10 min🍽 8 servings🔥 145 cal
AppetizerAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Katz Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 8 slices soft white bread
  • 1/2 cup softened butter
  • 1/2 cup hundreds and thousands

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Set out fresh soft white bread and use a small offset spatula to spread every slice evenly with butter.
  2. Step 2.Pour hundreds and thousands into a shallow plate wide enough to fit a bread slice.
  3. Step 3.Press the buttered side of one slice firmly into the sprinkles so the whole surface is covered.
  4. Step 4.Lift the bread carefully, shake away loose sprinkles, and repeat with the remaining slices.
  5. Step 5.Trim crusts only if desired, keeping the slices intact until all sprinkle coating is complete.
  6. Step 6.Cut each slice into neat triangles and serve straight away for the freshest texture and colour.

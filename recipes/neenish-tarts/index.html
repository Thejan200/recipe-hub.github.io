Neenish Tarts

Classic Australian Neenish tarts with jam, cream filling and signature two-tone icing.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Cool pastry shells completely before filling. Keep this in mind too: Runny icing will spill over the tarts.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 1 hr 25 min🍽 12 servings🔥 220 cal
DessertAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Ingredients

  • sweet shortcrust pastry
  • 1/2 cup raspberry jam
  • 1 cup thickened cream
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • pink food colouring

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Roll sweet pastry thinly, line tart tins and chill the shells so they hold their shape in the oven.
  2. Step 2.Blind-bake until crisp and golden, then cool completely before adding any filling or icing.
  3. Step 3.Spoon a small layer of raspberry jam into each shell to create the traditional fruity base.
  4. Step 4.Whip cream to firm peaks and pipe or spoon it over the jam without overfilling the pastry.
  5. Step 5.Make white icing, divide it, then tint one half pink and stir cocoa into the other half.
  6. Step 6.Ice half of each tart with pink and half with chocolate icing, meeting cleanly down the centre.

Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, fluffy cinnamon rolls layered with brown sugar filling and finished with tangy cream cheese icing.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup softened unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Warm the milk to about 105–110°F, stir in yeast and a pinch of sugar, and wait 5 to 10 minutes until a foamy cap confirms activity.
  2. Step 2.Whisk the remaining sugar, eggs, and cooled melted butter into the yeast mixture, then add flour and salt to form a shaggy dough.
  3. Step 3.Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky rather than wet, adding extra flour only one spoonful at a time.
  4. Step 4.Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let it rise in a warm draft-free place until doubled, about 60 to 90 minutes.
  5. Step 5.Roll the dough into an even rectangle, spread softened butter nearly to the edges, and cover it evenly with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  6. Step 6.Roll tightly from a long edge, pinch the seam, cut into 12 equal pieces, and arrange them cut-side up in a buttered baking dish.
  7. Step 7.Cover and rise 30 to 45 minutes until puffy and touching, then bake at 350°F for 23 to 28 minutes until golden and 190°F inside.
  8. Step 8.Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla smooth, cool the rolls 10 minutes, and spread icing while warm but not scorching hot.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Milk hotter than 120°F can damage yeast.
  • Use dental floss for clean slices.
  • An instant-read thermometer prevents doughy centers.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding too much flour creates dry rolls.
  • Loose rolling makes gaps in the spirals.
  • Icing immediately on oven-hot rolls makes it disappear.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use instant yeast and skip the foaming wait.
  • Use bread flour for a chewier roll.
  • Make a simple powdered-sugar glaze without cream cheese.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cover at room temperature for 1 day.
  • Refrigerate cream-cheese-iced rolls for up to 4 days.
  • Rewarm gently before serving.

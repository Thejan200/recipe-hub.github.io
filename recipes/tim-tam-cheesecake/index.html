Tim Tam Cheesecake

No-bake chocolate Tim Tam cheesecake with a cookie crumb base and creamy filling.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 4 hr 25 min🍽 10 servings🔥 470 cal
DessertAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: OnePotChefShow

Ingredients

  • 250 g Tim Tam biscuits
  • 90 g melted butter
  • 500 g cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup icing sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 300 ml thickened cream
  • extra Tim Tams

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Crush Tim Tams finely, combine with melted butter and press firmly into a lined springform pan.
  2. Step 2.Chill the base while you beat room-temperature cream cheese, icing sugar and vanilla until completely smooth.
  3. Step 3.Whip the cream to soft peaks in a separate bowl so it stays light when folded into the filling.
  4. Step 4.Fold cream through the cream-cheese mixture, then stir through chopped Tim Tam pieces gently.
  5. Step 5.Spread the filling over the chilled base, smooth the top and refrigerate until firm throughout.
  6. Step 6.Decorate with extra Tim Tams immediately before slicing with a warm knife for clean portions.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Let cream cheese soften before beating.

! Common Mistakes

  • Folding in over-whipped cream can make the filling grainy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use chocolate sandwich cookies if Tim Tams are unavailable.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate up to three days.

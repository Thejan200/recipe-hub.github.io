Breakfast Casserole

An oven-baked breakfast casserole of hash browns, bacon, softened vegetables, eggs, and cheddar, cut into warm shareable squares.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Thawing the potatoes avoids excess moisture in the base. Keep this in mind too: Wet, frozen potatoes can make the casserole watery.

Ingredients

  • 10 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 8 oz cooked bacon, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 2 cups frozen diced hash browns, thawed
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F and coat a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with butter.
  2. Step 2.Soften the onion and bell pepper in a skillet, cooking off visible moisture before they go into the dish.
  3. Step 3.Spread the thawed hash browns across the prepared dish.
  4. Step 4.Distribute the vegetables and bacon evenly over the potatoes.
  5. Step 5.Whisk eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until smooth and slightly aerated.
  6. Step 6.Pour the egg mixture slowly so it settles around the filling rather than sitting in one corner.
  7. Step 7.Scatter cheddar over the top in an even layer.
  8. Step 8.Bake until the edges are puffed and the middle no longer appears liquid.
  9. Step 9.Rest for about 10 minutes so the eggs firm enough for neat portions.
  10. Step 10.Cut into squares and serve warm with hot sauce or fresh herbs if wanted.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Thawing the potatoes avoids excess moisture in the base.
  • Cook watery vegetables before assembling the dish.
  • Take the casserole out once the centre is set to keep the eggs tender.

! Common Mistakes

  • Wet, frozen potatoes can make the casserole watery.
  • Overbaking gives eggs a rubbery texture.
  • Slicing immediately produces untidy portions.

↔ Substitutions

  • Cooked sausage can replace bacon.
  • Monterey Jack works instead of cheddar.
  • Use spinach only after wilting and squeezing it dry.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate covered leftovers for up to four days.
  • Reheat individual pieces gently.
  • Freeze cooled portions for up to two months.

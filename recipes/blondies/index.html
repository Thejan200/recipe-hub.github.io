Blondies

Soft-centred brown sugar bars with a light caramel note, vanilla aroma and a thin golden crust.

Ingredients

  • 140 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
  • 300 g light brown sugar
  • 1 large egg plus 1 egg yolk, at room temperature
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 155 g all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
  • 170 g white chocolate chunks or toasted pecans

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Set the oven to 175°C and line an 20 cm square pan with one long sheet of baking paper, leaving handles above two sides.
  2. Step 2.Stir the melted butter and brown sugar together until the sugar looks evenly moistened and no dry clumps remain around the bowl.
  3. Step 3.Whisk in the egg, yolk and vanilla until the mixture becomes smooth, thick and glossy; let it sit briefly if the butter was still warm.
  4. Step 4.Combine flour, baking powder and salt in a second bowl so the raising agent is spread through the flour before it reaches the wet mixture.
  5. Step 5.Fold the flour mixture into the butter base with a spatula, stopping as soon as the batter is uniform so the bars remain tender instead of bready.
  6. Step 6.Fold through the chosen chocolate or nuts, then press the batter into the prepared pan with a spatula dipped briefly in warm water.
  7. Step 7.Bake until the perimeter is set and golden while the centre still gives slightly when the pan is nudged; a dry centre means the bars have baked too long.
  8. Step 8.Leave the slab in its pan until completely cool, then lift using the paper handles and cut with a warm dry knife for neat, chewy squares.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Press brown sugar into the measuring cup or weigh it for a consistent moist texture.
  • The middle continues setting from residual heat, so remove the pan before it looks completely firm.
  • Wipe the knife between cuts if melted chocolate begins to drag across the surface.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding eggs to butter that is still steaming can create little cooked egg strands in the batter.
  • Leaving the pan in until a skewer emerges completely dry produces dry, cake-like bars.
  • Removing warm pieces from the pan can tear their soft centres and spoil the clean edges.

↔ Substitutions

  • Milk or dark chocolate may replace white chocolate in the same quantity.
  • Use toasted walnuts, pecans or pumpkin seeds for a nutty crunch.
  • Brown the butter first and cool it to warm, not hot, for a deeper toasted flavour.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep cut blondies in an airtight tin at room temperature for up to four days.
  • Chilling firms the bars; bring them back to room temperature for the softest bite.
  • Freeze separated squares in a sealed container for up to two months and thaw while wrapped.

Soft Gingerbread Cookies

Thick, tender gingerbread cookies with a soft molasses-rich middle, a cozy spice blend, and gently crinkled tops.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Measure molasses with a lightly oiled spoon so it releases cleanly. Keep this in mind too: Overbaking makes these cookies crisp rather than soft.

Published 2026-11-15

⏱ 1 hr 15 min🍽 24 servings🔥 145 cal
DessertUSAChristmas
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup light brown sugar, packed
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/3 cup unsulphured molasses
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar, for rolling

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Whisk the flour, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, baking soda and salt in a bowl so the spices are evenly distributed.
  2. Step 2.Beat the softened butter and brown sugar until creamy, then mix in the egg, molasses and vanilla until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
  3. Step 3.Stir in the dry ingredients just until a soft dough forms, cover it, and chill for 45 minutes so the cookies bake thick instead of spreading flat.
  4. Step 4.Heat the oven to 350°F, roll the dough into tablespoon-size balls, and roll each ball lightly in granulated sugar before placing on parchment-lined trays.
  5. Step 5.Bake until the edges are set and the centres still look soft, about 9 to 11 minutes; do not wait for the tops to become firm.
  6. Step 6.Let the cookies cool on the tray for five minutes before transferring to a rack, where the centres finish setting into their soft texture.

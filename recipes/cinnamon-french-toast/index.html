Cinnamon French Toast

Cinnamon French toast with thick bread, a lightly sweetened vanilla custard, and a golden butter-cooked exterior.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Mix the custard again before each batch so cinnamon does not settle. To avoid a common problem, remember: An excessive amount of cinnamon can form dry clumps on the surface.

Ingredients

  • 8 thick slices brioche or Texas toast
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Maple syrup, for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Lay the bread out briefly so the outer surface dries and can take the custard without becoming weak.
  2. Step 2.Whisk the eggs and milk until the mixture is uniform.
  3. Step 3.Add cinnamon, vanilla, brown sugar, and salt, whisking until the sugar is mostly dispersed.
  4. Step 4.Warm a wide skillet over medium-low heat and coat it lightly with butter.
  5. Step 5.Dip each slice, turn it once, and let both sides take on the cinnamon custard.
  6. Step 6.Hold the slice over the dish for a moment so extra custard does not collect in the pan.
  7. Step 7.Cook in batches until the first surface is deeply golden.
  8. Step 8.Turn and cook until the second side is browned and the middle feels softly set.
  9. Step 9.Use fresh butter between batches and stir the custard before each new slice to redistribute the cinnamon.
  10. Step 10.Serve hot with maple syrup and more cinnamon if desired.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Mix the custard again before each batch so cinnamon does not settle.
  • Thick bread gives the middle time to become custardy without falling apart.
  • Medium-low heat helps both the bread and egg mixture cook evenly.

! Common Mistakes

  • An excessive amount of cinnamon can form dry clumps on the surface.
  • Soft bread becomes fragile when soaked for too long.
  • Burnt butter leaves bitter dark marks on the toast.

↔ Substitutions

  • Challah is a suitable alternative to brioche.
  • Light brown sugar or granulated sugar both work in the custard.
  • A small pinch of nutmeg adds another warm spice note.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooked slices for up to three days.
  • Reheat in a skillet or toaster oven.
  • Freeze cooled cooked slices for up to two months.

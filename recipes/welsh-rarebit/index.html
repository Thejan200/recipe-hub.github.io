Welsh Rarebit

A hot Welsh Rarebit topping of mature cheddar, mustard, butter, and ale or milk spooned over crisp buttered toast.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Crisp toast gives the rich topping a firm base. Keep this in mind too: Soft bread quickly turns soggy under the sauce.

⏱ 20 min🍽 3 servings🔥 430 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French or Italian bread, enough for 6 slices
  • 225 g good-quality cheddar cheese
  • 30 g butter, plus extra for the toast
  • 2 tbsp milk or ale
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Optional salt, cayenne pepper, garlic, or horseradish, to taste

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Toast the bread until crisp and butter it while it is hot.
  2. Step 2.Grate or finely chop the cheddar so it can melt evenly.
  3. Step 3.Melt the butter in a small pan over gentle heat, then add the cheese and stir continuously.
  4. Step 4.Pour in milk or ale gradually, keeping the mixture below a hard boil so the cheese remains smooth.
  5. Step 5.Stir in mustard and any optional seasoning, tasting as you go.
  6. Step 6.Spoon the topping over the toast and serve straight away.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Crisp toast gives the rich topping a firm base.
  • Mature cheddar gives the sauce its strongest flavour.
  • If using a grill finish, stop when the topping bubbles rather than browns heavily.

! Common Mistakes

  • Soft bread quickly turns soggy under the sauce.
  • High heat can split the cheese mixture.
  • Too much ale leaves the topping thin.

↔ Substitutions

  • Milk can replace ale.
  • Dijon produces a milder mustard character.
  • Omit cayenne for a less spicy topping.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Serve assembled rarebit immediately.
  • Refrigerate unused cheese topping for up to two days.
  • Reheat gently under a grill rather than in a microwave.

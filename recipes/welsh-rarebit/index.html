Welsh Rarebit

Buttered toast topped with a smooth cheddar, ale, butter, and mustard cheese sauce, served hot.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 20 min🍽 3 servings🔥 430 cal
AppetizerUK
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Tasting History with Max Miller

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French or Italian bread, enough for 6 slices
  • 225 g good-quality cheddar cheese
  • 30 g butter, plus extra for the toast
  • 2 tbsp milk or ale
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Optional salt, cayenne pepper, garlic, or horseradish, to taste

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Slice six pieces of bread, toast them in a toaster or under a broiler, and butter each slice lightly while hot.
  2. Step 2.Chop the cheddar into small pieces or grate it so that it melts evenly.
  3. Step 3.Melt the measured butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the cheese and stir constantly as it melts.
  4. Step 4.Gradually stir in the milk or ale, keeping the heat gentle so the cheese does not bubble, burn, or split.
  5. Step 5.Stir in the mustard and any optional seasonings to taste.
  6. Step 6.Spoon the hot cheese sauce over the buttered toast and serve immediately for the best texture.

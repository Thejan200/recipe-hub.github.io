Eton Mess

Crisp vanilla meringue, juicy strawberries, and softly whipped cream folded into a classic British dessert.

⏱ 180 min🍽 6 servings🔥 390 cal

Ingredients

  • 4 large egg whites, at room temperature
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 cup (200 g) superfine sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, divided
  • 1 1/2 lb (675 g) strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 1/3 cup plus 2 tbsp (90 g) superfine sugar, divided
  • 2 cups (480 ml) heavy whipping cream

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Preheat the oven to 200°F (95°C), line a baking sheet with parchment, and make sure the mixing bowl and whisk are grease-free.
  2. Step 2.Beat the egg whites, cream of tartar, and salt until foamy, then gradually add the 200 grams sugar while beating to glossy stiff peaks.
  3. Step 3.Beat in one-half teaspoon vanilla, spread or pipe the meringue on the prepared sheet, and keep the layer reasonably even for uniform drying.
  4. Step 4.Bake for 1 hour, turn off the oven, and leave the meringue inside for 1 to 2 hours until crisp and completely cool.
  5. Step 5.Toss the strawberries with the one-third cup portion of sugar, lightly crush some berries, and rest them until a bright syrup develops.
  6. Step 6.Whip the cold cream with the remaining 2 tablespoons sugar and one-half teaspoon vanilla to soft peaks that still fold easily.
  7. Step 7.Break the cooled meringue into bite-size pieces, reserving a few attractive shards, and fold most of it gently through the whipped cream.
  8. Step 8.Fold in the strawberries and their syrup only a few times for visible ribbons, then portion immediately and garnish with the reserved meringue.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Add sugar gradually for a stable meringue.
  • Cool the meringue fully before assembly.
  • Fold briefly to keep the dessert marbled.

! Common Mistakes

  • Grease in the bowl prevents egg whites whipping.
  • Warm meringue melts the cream.
  • Assembling too early softens every crisp piece.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use raspberries for part of the strawberries.
  • Caster sugar may replace superfine sugar.
  • Prepare the meringue a day ahead and keep it airtight.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Serve immediately after assembly.
  • Store dry meringue airtight for up to 3 days.
  • Refrigerate whipped cream and berries separately for up to 1 day.

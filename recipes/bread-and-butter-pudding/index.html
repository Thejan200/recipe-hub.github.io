Bread and Butter Pudding

Buttered bread and sultanas baked in a vanilla-nutmeg custard until golden and softly set.

Published 2026-09-20

⏱ 55 min🍽 8 servings🔥 360 cal
DessertUKChristmas
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: KeefCooks

Ingredients

  • 8 slices stale white bread
  • 75 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 large eggs
  • 200 ml whole milk
  • 100 ml double or heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp demerara sugar
  • 60 g sultanas
  • Ground nutmeg, for a light sprinkle
  • Vanilla essence, one small splash

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Butter the baking dish lightly with part of the measured butter, then spread the remaining butter generously over one side of every bread slice.
  2. Step 2.Cut the bread into triangles and arrange about half in a slightly overlapping base layer; scatter over half the sultanas and a light grating of nutmeg.
  3. Step 3.Add a second bread, sultana, and nutmeg layer, reserving 4 to 6 triangles for an exposed top and tucking in fruit that might burn.
  4. Step 4.Warm the milk and double cream together until just below boiling, then remove the pan from the heat before it bubbles.
  5. Step 5.Reserve 1 tablespoon from the measured demerara sugar for the surface, whisk the remaining 2 tablespoons with the eggs, and add the vanilla.
  6. Step 6.Whisk the warm milk-and-cream mixture gradually into the eggs, then pour the custard into every cavity and press the bread gently until moistened.
  7. Step 7.Scatter the reserved tablespoon of sugar over the top and let the pudding stand while the oven heats to 160°C fan or 180°C conventional.
  8. Step 8.Bake for 40 minutes until puffed, golden, and just set in the middle, then rest for several minutes before serving hot or cold.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Slightly stale bread absorbs custard without collapsing.
  • Butter each slice to the edges.
  • Allow a short soak before baking.

! Common Mistakes

  • Fresh soft bread can turn mushy.
  • Pouring custard too quickly leaves dry pockets.
  • Overbaking removes the soft custardy center.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use raisins in place of sultanas at the same weight.
  • Use double cream or heavy cream.
  • Use pure vanilla extract as the vanilla essence equivalent.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate covered for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat portions until steaming hot.
  • Freeze cooled portions for up to 1 month.

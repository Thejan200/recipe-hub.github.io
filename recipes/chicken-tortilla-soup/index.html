Chicken Tortilla Soup

A vibrant chicken tortilla soup with black beans, corn, fire-roasted tomatoes, warm spices, and crunchy tortilla strips added at the table.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Shredded rotisserie chicken makes this soup especially quick to assemble. Keep this in mind too: Leaving already-cooked chicken at a hard simmer for too long can dry it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 3 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 6 corn tortillas, cut into strips
  • Avocado, cilantro, and lime wedges, for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a soup pot over medium heat and cook the onion and jalapeño until tender, about 6 minutes.
  2. Step 2.Add garlic, cumin, and chili powder, stirring for about 30 seconds so the spices become fragrant without burning.
  3. Step 3.Pour in the fire-roasted tomatoes and chicken broth, loosening any flavourful bits on the bottom of the pot as the liquid warms.
  4. Step 4.Stir in black beans and corn, then simmer partly covered for 15 minutes to bring the broth together.
  5. Step 5.Add the cooked shredded chicken and warm it through for about 5 minutes rather than cooking it for a long time.
  6. Step 6.In a separate wide skillet, heat the remaining oil and fry the tortilla strips in batches until crisp and golden. Drain and lightly salt them.
  7. Step 7.Stir lime juice into the soup, then taste and make small adjustments to salt, cumin, or lime as needed.
  8. Step 8.Serve the soup with avocado, cilantro, lime wedges, and tortilla strips added at the last moment.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Shredded rotisserie chicken makes this soup especially quick to assemble.
  • Keep the tortilla strips separate until serving so they remain crisp.
  • Removing jalapeño seeds and membranes reduces the heat while keeping its fresh pepper flavour.

! Common Mistakes

  • Leaving already-cooked chicken at a hard simmer for too long can dry it out.
  • Adding tortilla strips early lets them absorb broth and lose their crunch.
  • Skipping the rinse on canned beans can cloud the broth.

↔ Substitutions

  • Pinto beans are a suitable alternative to black beans.
  • Use cooked turkey in place of chicken when available.
  • For baked strips, toss tortillas lightly with oil and bake at 400°F until crisp, watching closely.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate the cooled soup base without toppings for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat until hot throughout, then finish each bowl with fresh toppings.
  • Freeze the soup base for up to 3 months; make tortilla strips and add avocado after reheating.

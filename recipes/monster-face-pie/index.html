Monster Face Pie

A golden vegetable-and-beef pie decorated with an expressive, kid-friendly monster face for a Halloween dinner table.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Cool the filling briefly so the bottom pastry stays crisp. One thing to watch for is A wet filling can split the pastry and make the base soggy.

⏱ 1 hr 5 min🍽 6 servings🔥 485 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 500 g lean ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 2 sheets ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • sliced olives, cheese and peppers for the face

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 200°C and line a baking tray with baking paper so the finished pie can be moved without disturbing its decorated top.
  2. Step 2.Warm the olive oil in a skillet, then cook the onion and carrot with a pinch of salt until the vegetables soften and smell sweet rather than raw.
  3. Step 3.Add the beef and break it into small pieces, cooking until no pink remains before stirring in tomato paste, thyme and black pepper.
  4. Step 4.Pour in the stock and simmer until the filling is moist but thick enough to mound on a spoon; cool it for ten minutes before it meets the pastry.
  5. Step 5.Place one pastry sheet on the tray, spoon the cooled filling into the centre, and leave a clean border so the two pastry layers can seal properly.
  6. Step 6.Brush the border with beaten egg, place the second sheet over the filling, and crimp the edges firmly with a fork to keep steam and juices inside.
  7. Step 7.Use olives, cheese and pepper pieces to make a cheerful monster face, then cut a few small vents away from the decorations so the pastry can release steam.
  8. Step 8.Brush the exposed pastry with egg and bake until deeply golden and crisp, then rest the pie for ten minutes before cutting clean slices.

! Common Mistakes

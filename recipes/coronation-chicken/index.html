Coronation Chicken

Chicken in a mild curried mayonnaise and crème fraîche sauce with apricots and sultanas.

⏱ 40 min🍽 4 servings🔥 430 cal

Ingredients

  • 2–3 chicken breasts
  • 500 ml chicken stock
  • A large knob of butter (or oil) for frying
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1–2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 60 ml red wine
  • 60 ml water
  • 200 g mayonnaise
  • 125 g crème fraîche
  • 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp dried apricots, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sultanas
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Poach the chicken breasts in the chicken stock with the diced onion and bay leaves until cooked through, then cool the chicken and reserve the stock for another use.
  2. Step 2.Fry the onion in the butter or oil until softened, then stir in the curry powder, turmeric, brown sugar, and tomato purée.
  3. Step 3.Add the red wine and water, simmer the curry base briefly, then let it cool completely.
  4. Step 4.Mix the cooled curry base with the mayonnaise, crème fraîche, and lemon juice.
  5. Step 5.Fold in the cooled chicken, chopped dried apricots, and sultanas.
  6. Step 6.Season with salt and pepper to taste, then chill before serving.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cool the curry base completely before adding the mayonnaise and crème fraîche.
  • Cool the poached chicken before folding it into the sauce.
  • Fold in the apricots and sultanas gently at the end.

! Common Mistakes

  • Warm curry base can loosen the mayonnaise and crème fraîche sauce.
  • Adding the dairy sauce before the curry base cools can affect its texture.
  • Skipping the lemon juice can leave the finished sauce tasting flat.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use oil instead of butter for frying the onion.
  • Adjust the salt and pepper only after the sauce is fully mixed.
  • Use one or two bay leaves, as preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 2 days.
  • Keep covered and chilled.
  • Do not freeze mayonnaise-based salad.

Traditional Wiener Schnitzel

A classic, very thin veal cutlet in a light golden crumb, served with lemon and parsley potatoes.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Fine crumbs and a gently moving pan make the coating lighter. One thing to watch for is Pressing crumbs tightly onto the veal prevents the crust from lifting.

Ingredients

  • 4 veal cutlets
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups fine breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
  • 1/2 cup neutral oil
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Pat the veal dry and place each cutlet between baking paper, then pound it evenly until thin enough to cook quickly without becoming ragged around the edges.
  2. Step 2.Season both sides modestly with salt and white pepper, then arrange flour, lightly beaten egg, and fine crumbs in three separate shallow dishes.
  3. Step 3.Dredge a cutlet in flour and shake it clean, pass it through egg, then lay it in breadcrumbs without pressing the coating hard into the veal.
  4. Step 4.Heat the oil and butter in a broad skillet until the butter foam settles, choosing a pan large enough that the cutlets can move slightly in the fat.
  5. Step 5.Fry one or two cutlets at a time, nudging the pan so the hot fat washes over the surface and helps the crumb form its characteristic rippled finish.
  6. Step 6.Turn when the underside is golden, then cook the second side briefly until the veal is just done and the crust is crisp rather than dark brown.
  7. Step 7.Drain on a rack for a moment and sprinkle with salt while still hot; avoid stacking the cutlets because trapped steam softens the coating.
  8. Step 8.Serve immediately with lemon, parsley potatoes, and a fresh salad, keeping the presentation simple so the crisp veal stays the focus.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Fine crumbs and a gently moving pan make the coating lighter.

! Common Mistakes

  • Pressing crumbs tightly onto the veal prevents the crust from lifting.

↔ Substitutions

  • For a different dish, use chicken cutlets; the traditional name refers to veal.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool promptly and refrigerate for up to 2 days; reheat uncovered in the oven.

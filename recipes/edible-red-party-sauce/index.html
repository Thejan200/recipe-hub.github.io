Edible Red Party Sauce

A bright berry dipping sauce with a glossy finish for fruit, cookies and Halloween dessert boards.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Use frozen berries directly; thawing is unnecessary. A common setback is Boiling hard after thickening can make the sauce dull and overly sticky.

⏱ 17 min🍽 10 servings🔥 65 cal

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water
  • pinch of salt

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Combine the berries, sugar, lemon juice and salt in a small saucepan and warm over medium heat until the fruit starts to release its juices.
  2. Step 2.Press larger berries gently with a spoon so the sauce becomes textured and spoonable while still keeping small pieces for a natural fruit flavour.
  3. Step 3.Whisk the cornstarch with cold water in a separate cup until completely smooth; adding dry starch directly to hot fruit makes hard lumps.
  4. Step 4.Stir the slurry into the simmering berries and continue stirring until the sauce turns glossy and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  5. Step 5.Remove the pan from the heat and cool the sauce for ten minutes, because it will thicken further as the natural fruit pectin sets.
  6. Step 6.For a smoother dip, push the warm sauce through a fine sieve; for a rustic party bowl, leave the berry pieces in place.
  7. Step 7.Spoon into a clean serving bowl and surround it with apple slices, strawberries, cookies or cake cubes just before guests arrive.
  8. Step 8.Refrigerate leftovers promptly and rewarm gently if a softer dipping consistency is wanted the next day.

  • Use frozen berries directly; thawing is unnecessary.

! Common Mistakes

↔ Substitutions

  • Refrigerate in a sealed container for up to five days.

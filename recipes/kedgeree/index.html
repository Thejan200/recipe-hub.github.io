Kedgeree

Gently spiced rice with smoked haddock, peas, herbs, and boiled eggs for a classic Anglo-Indian breakfast dish.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 45 min🍽 4 servings🔥 520 cal
BreakfastUK

Ingredients

  • 300 g smoked haddock
  • 300 ml milk
  • 250 g basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 3 boiled eggs, quartered
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Poach haddock gently in milk.
  • Rinse rice before cooking.
  • Fold fish through carefully so it stays flaky.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling fish hard makes it dry.
  • Wet rice makes kedgeree heavy.
  • Aggressive stirring breaks the eggs and fish.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use smoked cod if haddock is unavailable.
  • Use coriander instead of parsley.
  • Add a little chilli only if desired.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat rice thoroughly until steaming.
  • Do not leave cooked rice at room temperature.

Kedgeree

Fragrant rice folded with gently poached smoked fish, peas, lemon, herbs and egg for a comforting Anglo-Indian breakfast-style meal.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Cooling the rice briefly after cooking helps keep the grains separate in the pan. A common setback is Overcooked rice turns sticky when folded with the fish.

Ingredients

  • 300 g smoked haddock
  • 300 ml milk
  • 250 g basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 3 boiled eggs, quartered
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Rinse the rice in several changes of cold water until the water is mostly clear. Cook it according to the packet directions, then spread it on a tray or large plate so excess steam can escape.
  2. Step 2.Place the haddock in a shallow pan, pour over the milk, and warm it over low heat until the fish flakes at the thickest point. Lift the fish out, remove any skin or bones, and break it into large pieces.
  3. Step 3.Melt the butter in a wide pan over medium heat. Cook the onion until translucent, then stir in the curry powder and turmeric for a few seconds.
  4. Step 4.Add the cooked rice and peas. Fold rather than stir vigorously, warming everything until the peas are hot and the rice is evenly yellow.
  5. Step 5.Add the haddock, parsley and lemon juice. Season cautiously because smoked fish can be salty, then fold just enough to keep the fish in distinct flakes.
  6. Step 6.Spoon the kedgeree onto warm plates and arrange the egg quarters over the top.
  7. Step 7.Serve straight away while the fish and rice are hot.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cooling the rice briefly after cooking helps keep the grains separate in the pan.
  • Poach the fish at a low temperature rather than boiling the milk.
  • Taste before salting because smoked haddock varies greatly in saltiness.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overcooked rice turns sticky when folded with the fish.
  • Boiling the haddock aggressively makes it dry and tough.
  • Stirring hard breaks the fish into a paste.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use smoked cod or another firm smoked white fish in place of haddock.
  • Use coriander or dill instead of parsley.
  • Add a pinch of mild chilli only if you want extra heat.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use them within 2 days.
  • Reheat until piping hot, adding a splash of water if the rice is dry.
  • Do not keep cooked rice at room temperature.

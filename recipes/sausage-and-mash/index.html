Sausage and Mash

Browned pork sausages served with creamy mashed potatoes and a rich onion gravy for a classic British comfort meal.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 50 min🍽 2 servings🔥 760 cal

Ingredients

  • 6 English pork sausages
  • 450 g potatoes, peeled and chunked
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp butter for mash
  • 500 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp butter for gravy
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 medium onions, sliced
  • 1 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • Salt and white pepper to taste
  • Oil for frying the sausages

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Peel and chunk the potatoes; slice the onions. Put the potatoes in salted water and simmer until they yield easily to a fork.
  2. Step 2.While the potatoes cook, brown the sausages in a little oil over medium heat, turning until evenly cooked through. Keep warm.
  3. Step 3.Melt the gravy butter in the sausage pan and slowly soften the onions until golden. Stir in the sugar and thyme.
  4. Step 4.Sprinkle flour over the onions and cook briefly. Gradually add the beef stock, stirring until smooth; add Worcestershire sauce and simmer until the gravy coats a spoon.
  5. Step 5.Drain the potatoes well. Mash with the milk and butter, then season with salt and white pepper.
  6. Step 6.Serve three sausages per person over warm mash with onion gravy spooned on top.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use a heavy pan so the sausages brown evenly.
  • Warm the milk before adding it to mash.
  • Cook onions slowly for a deeper gravy.

! Common Mistakes

  • Undercooked sausages are unsafe; cook thoroughly.
  • Rushing the onions leaves the gravy sharp.
  • Too much liquid makes the mash watery.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use Cumberland sausages for a traditional flavor.
  • Use chicken stock for a lighter gravy.
  • Use olive oil instead of vegetable oil.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate sausages, mash and gravy separately for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat each component thoroughly before serving.

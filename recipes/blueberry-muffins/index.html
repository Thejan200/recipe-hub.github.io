Blueberry Muffins

Tall bakery-style blueberry muffins with tender vanilla crumb, juicy berries, and crisp sugared tops.

Published 2026-09-19

⏱ 42 min🍽 12 servings🔥 250 cal
DessertUSABreakfast

Video: Preppy Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 1/2 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tbsp flour, for berries
  • 2 tbsp coarse sugar

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 400°F, line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners, and lightly grease the exposed top surface to prevent sticking.
  2. Step 2.Whisk two cups flour, baking powder, and salt until evenly combined so no concentrated pocket of leavener remains in the batter.
  3. Step 3.Beat softened butter and sugar until noticeably lighter and fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a time and add the vanilla.
  4. Step 4.Blend in sour cream and milk on low speed; the mixture may look slightly curdled, which will smooth out when the flour is folded in.
  5. Step 5.Fold the dry mixture into the wet ingredients only until a few flour streaks remain, avoiding vigorous mixing that develops tough gluten.
  6. Step 6.Toss the blueberries with one tablespoon flour, fold them gently into the thick batter, and reserve a few berries for visible muffin tops.
  7. Step 7.Divide batter evenly, filling the cups nearly full, add the reserved berries and coarse sugar, and bake until the domes turn golden.
  8. Step 8.Check at 20 to 24 minutes with a tester away from a berry; cool in the pan 5 minutes, then move muffins to a rack to prevent soggy bottoms.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Do not thaw frozen blueberries before folding them in.
  • Room-temperature dairy blends more evenly.
  • A high initial oven temperature encourages tall domes.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overmixing produces tunnels and a tough crumb.
  • A tester pushed through a berry can falsely look underbaked.
  • Leaving muffins in the hot pan traps steam.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use plain Greek yogurt instead of sour cream.
  • Replace blueberries with raspberries.
  • Use lemon zest for a citrus variation.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep airtight at room temperature for 2 days.
  • Refrigerate for up to 5 days.
  • Freeze cooled muffins for up to 2 months.

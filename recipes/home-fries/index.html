Home Fries

Golden breakfast potato cubes tossed with tender onion, red pepper, smoked paprika, and parsley, with browned sides and a soft centre.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Put the potato cubes in a saucepan, cover with lightly salted water, and bring them just to a low boil.
  2. Step 2.Cook until a knife meets only slight resistance at the outside of a cube, about 5 to 6 minutes, then drain them completely.
  3. Step 3.Leave the potatoes in the colander for a few minutes so residual steam dries their surfaces.
  4. Step 4.Heat a broad, heavy skillet over medium-high heat and coat the base with avocado oil.
  5. Step 5.Tip in the potatoes and arrange them across the pan with space where possible; do not stir at first.
  6. Step 6.Once the bottom faces are browned, turn sections of potatoes and continue until several surfaces have colour.
  7. Step 7.Make a clear patch in the pan, melt in the butter, and add the onion and red pepper to that spot.
  8. Step 8.Cook the vegetables until they soften and pick up a little colour, then mix them through the potatoes.
  9. Step 9.Sprinkle over the paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, and turn gently so the seasoning reaches every cube.
  10. Step 10.Give the mixture a final few minutes in the skillet, checking that the potato centres are tender before adding parsley and serving.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Start with similarly sized cubes so they soften and brown at the same pace.
  • Letting the drained potatoes shed steam helps the oil brown them rather than trapping moisture.
  • Choose the widest skillet available so the potatoes have contact with the hot surface.

! Common Mistakes

  • Filling the pan too deeply leaves the potatoes soft because steam cannot escape.
  • Turning every minute interrupts the crust that gives home fries their colour.
  • Cooking the potatoes until fully soft in water makes them prone to breaking apart in the skillet.

↔ Substitutions

  • Yukon Gold potatoes make a richer, more buttery-tasting alternative to russets.
  • A green or yellow bell pepper can stand in for the red pepper.
  • Bacon drippings can replace avocado oil for a deeper savoury flavour.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled home fries in a covered container for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat them in a lightly oiled hot skillet until the edges are crisp again.
  • They can be frozen for up to 1 month, although freshly cooked potatoes have the best contrast of textures.

Seafood Chowder

A creamy Nova Scotia-style seafood chowder with fish and shellfish.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 50 min🍽 6 servings🔥 430 cal
SoupCanadaSeafoodDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: The Canteen Cooks

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 3 cups fish stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 8 ounces white fish, cubed
  • 8 ounces shrimp, peeled
  • 8 ounces scallops
  • fresh parsley
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Melt butter in a heavy pot over medium heat.
  2. Step 2.Cook onion and celery until softened.
  3. Step 3.Add potatoes and fish stock, then simmer until almost tender.
  4. Step 4.Stir in cream and milk and keep the heat gentle.
  5. Step 5.Add the fish and cook until nearly opaque.
  6. Step 6.Add shrimp and scallops in the final minutes.
  7. Step 7.Season and simmer only until all seafood is just cooked.
  8. Step 8.Rest for five minutes, then serve with parsley.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Measure and prepare every ingredient before heating the pan.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end of cooking.
  • Let the finished dish rest briefly before serving.

! Common Mistakes

  • Rushing the browning or simmering stage.
  • Overcrowding the pan or pot.
  • Skipping the final seasoning check.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use an equivalent stock when the listed stock is unavailable.
  • Use a similar fresh herb if the listed herb is unavailable.
  • Choose a comparable cut or seafood of the same cooking time.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool leftovers within two hours and refrigerate in an airtight container.
  • Reheat gently until piping hot throughout.
  • Freeze only fully cooled portions in freezer-safe containers.

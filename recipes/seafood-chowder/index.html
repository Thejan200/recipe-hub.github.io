Seafood Chowder

A creamy Nova Scotia-style seafood chowder with fish and shellfish.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 50 min🍽 6 servings🔥 430 cal
SoupCanadaSeafoodDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: The Canteen Cooks

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 3 cups fish stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 8 ounces white fish, cubed
  • 8 ounces shrimp, peeled
  • 8 ounces scallops
  • fresh parsley
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Melt butter in a heavy pot over medium heat.
  2. Step 2.Cook onion and celery until softened.
  3. Step 3.Add potatoes and fish stock, then simmer until almost tender.
  4. Step 4.Stir in cream and milk and keep the heat gentle.
  5. Step 5.Add the fish and cook until nearly opaque.
  6. Step 6.Add shrimp and scallops in the final minutes.
  7. Step 7.Season and simmer only until all seafood is just cooked.
  8. Step 8.Rest for five minutes, then serve with parsley.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Prepare and measure seafood before starting because shellfish cooks quickly once it reaches the pot.
  • Add firm fish first and tender shellfish last so each type stays tender.
  • Taste before adding salt because seafood stock, bacon, and shellfish can already be salty.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding all seafood at once and overcooking smaller pieces.
  • Boiling the chowder after cream is added.
  • Using seafood that has a strong off smell; use fresh, properly stored ingredients only.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use the seafood that is fresh and appropriate for your area, keeping cooking times suited to each type.
  • Use corn or diced fennel in place of part of the potato for a different flavour.
  • Use a fish or vegetable stock that suits the seafood you choose.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled chowder for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat gently until hot throughout; avoid repeated reheating.
  • Do not freeze a creamy chowder if you want the best seafood and potato texture.

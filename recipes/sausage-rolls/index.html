Sausage Rolls

Flaky Australian sausage rolls with a savoury pork-and-beef filling and crisp pastry.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Keep the puff pastry chilled until you are ready to shape it. To avoid a common problem, remember: Overfilling the pastry so the rolls split as they bake.

⏱ 45 min🍽 12 servings🔥 240 cal

Ingredients

  • 500 g sausage meat
  • 250 g beef mince
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg yolk, beaten
  • sesame seeds, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Combine sausage meat, beef mince, grated onion, grated carrot, breadcrumbs and egg in a bowl until the mixture is evenly blended.
  2. Step 2.Place one sheet of puff pastry on a cool work surface and cut it in half lengthways to make two long rectangles.
  3. Step 3.Shape one quarter of the filling into a log along the long edge of a pastry rectangle, leaving a narrow border for sealing.
  4. Step 4.Brush the exposed pastry edge with water, roll the pastry firmly around the filling, and place the roll seam-side down.
  5. Step 5.Repeat with the remaining pastry and filling, then cut each long roll into even pieces with a sharp knife so the pastry is not crushed.
  6. Step 6.Arrange the rolls with space between them on a lined baking tray, brush with beaten egg yolk and scatter sesame seeds if using.
  7. Step 7.Bake until the pastry is puffed, crisp and richly golden and the filling is completely cooked through at the centre.
  8. Step 8.Rest for five minutes before serving with tomato sauce, remembering that the filling stays extremely hot immediately from the oven.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep the puff pastry chilled until you are ready to shape it.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overfilling the pastry so the rolls split as they bake.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use all pork sausage meat if you prefer a softer filling.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for three days or freeze baked rolls for up to two months.

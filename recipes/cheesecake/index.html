Cheesecake

A classic baked vanilla cheesecake with a compact graham base and a chilled, creamy centre.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Let the cream cheese and eggs lose their refrigerator chill before mixing for a smoother batter. Keep this in mind too: Using cold cream cheese can leave small lumps that do not bake out.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 24 ounces block cream cheese, at room temperature
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • Fresh berries, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 325°F. Grease a 9-inch springform pan; if you plan to use a water bath, cover the outside with a snug layer of foil.
  2. Step 2.Mix the graham crumbs with butter and sugar until the texture is evenly sandy. Pack the mixture over the base and a short way up the pan wall.
  3. Step 3.Bake the crust for 10 minutes, then place the pan on a rack to cool while the filling is prepared.
  4. Step 4.Beat the cream cheese with sugar and salt on medium-low speed, stopping to scrape the bowl and paddle until no visible lumps remain.
  5. Step 5.Add the eggs one at a time on low speed. Mix only until each one disappears into the batter so you do not add unnecessary air.
  6. Step 6.Blend in the sour cream and vanilla, then pour the filling over the cooled crust. Smooth the surface with a spatula.
  7. Step 7.Bake for 55 to 65 minutes. The outside should appear set while the central area still moves slightly as a single mass when the pan is carefully nudged.
  8. Step 8.Let the cheesecake cool gradually, then refrigerate it for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight. Release the pan only once it is cold and slice with a warmed, wiped knife.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Let the cream cheese and eggs lose their refrigerator chill before mixing for a smoother batter.
  • Keep the mixer on a low setting once the eggs are added to limit excess air in the filling.
  • The extended chill is part of the recipe: it firms the centre and makes the flavour more settled.

! Common Mistakes

  • Using cold cream cheese can leave small lumps that do not bake out.
  • Beating the batter hard after the eggs are added can lead to a tall rise followed by surface cracks.
  • Trying to portion a warm cheesecake produces untidy, soft slices.

↔ Substitutions

  • Vanilla wafer crumbs may be used in place of graham crumbs for a different crust flavour.
  • Full-fat Greek yogurt is a workable substitute for sour cream.
  • Add berries, caramel, or chocolate only after the cheesecake has fully chilled.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep the cheesecake covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
  • Freeze individual slices tightly wrapped for up to 2 months.
  • Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator and serve them chilled.

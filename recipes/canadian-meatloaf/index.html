Canadian Meatloaf

Juicy ketchup-glazed beef meatloaf for a comforting Canadian dinner.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 75 min🍽 8 servings🔥 390 cal
BeefCanadaDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup dry bread crumbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/3 cup ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons prepared mustard

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Step 2.Mix beef, egg, onion, milk, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper gently.
  3. Step 3.Shape the mixture into a loaf in a lined pan.
  4. Step 4.Whisk ketchup, brown sugar and mustard for the glaze.
  5. Step 5.Spread half the glaze over the loaf.
  6. Step 6.Bake until the centre reaches 160°F (71°C).
  7. Step 7.Brush with remaining glaze during the final minutes.
  8. Step 8.Rest ten minutes before slicing and serving.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Mix the meat mixture gently just until combined so the loaf stays tender.
  • Use a thermometer in the centre to confirm doneness rather than relying only on colour.
  • Rest the loaf before slicing so it holds together cleanly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overmixing the meat, which can make the loaf dense.
  • Packing the mixture too tightly into the pan.
  • Adding the glaze too early and letting the sugars darken before the loaf is cooked.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use ground turkey or a beef-pork blend, adjusting moisture if the meat is very lean.
  • Use oats in place of breadcrumbs when suitable for your dietary needs.
  • Use barbecue sauce or a tomato-based chutney in place of ketchup for a different glaze.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled meatloaf for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat slices covered with a splash of water or sauce to reduce drying.
  • Freeze individual cooled slices for convenient portions.

Ratloaf Meatloaf

A savory mouse-shaped meatloaf with herb potatoes and roasted vegetables for a playful Halloween supper.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Use a thermometer rather than judging doneness from color. One thing to watch for is Packing the mixture tightly produces a tough loaf.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 1 hr 5 min🍽 6 servings🔥 315 cal
DinnerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • olive slices and chive stems
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and line a rimmed pan so any rendered fat stays contained.
  2. Step 2.Mix beef, egg, crumbs, onion, ketchup, Worcestershire, salt and pepper only until combined; heavy mixing makes meatloaf dense.
  3. Step 3.Shape the mixture into a long teardrop with a rounded nose and a narrower tail end, then place it on the lined pan.
  4. Step 4.Bake until the centre reaches 71°C on an instant-read thermometer, then rest ten minutes before adding the olive eyes and chive whiskers.
  5. Step 5.Slice with a sharp knife and serve with roasted vegetables or mashed potatoes while the decorative details stay in place.

Salisbury Steak

Tender seasoned beef patties served in a rich mushroom-and-onion brown gravy for a classic American comfort-food dinner.

Published 2026-09-10

⏱ 55 min🍽 4 servings🔥 560 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups low-sodium beef broth

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Combine the ground beef, panko, egg, ketchup, Worcestershire, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a bowl, mixing gently with your hands only until the ingredients are evenly incorporated.
  2. Step 2.Divide the mixture into four equal portions and shape each into an oval patty about 3/4 inch thick, keeping the mixture loose enough that the cooked patties remain tender.
  3. Step 3.Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat, add the patties, and sear for about 3 minutes per side until deeply browned; transfer them to a clean plate.
  4. Step 4.Reduce the heat to medium, add the sliced mushrooms and onion to the same skillet, and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until softened and golden around the edges.
  5. Step 5.Add the butter, let it melt, then sprinkle in the flour and stir for about 1 minute so the flour coats the vegetables and loses its raw taste.
  6. Step 6.Gradually pour in the beef broth while stirring and scraping the browned fond from the bottom of the skillet, then bring the gravy to a gentle simmer until slightly thickened.
  7. Step 7.Return the browned patties and any collected juices to the skillet, spoon some gravy over the tops, and reduce the heat so the liquid maintains a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil.
  8. Step 8.Cover loosely and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, turning the patties once if necessary, until the centers reach 160°F and the gravy has a smooth, spoon-coating consistency.
  9. Step 9.Taste the gravy and adjust salt and pepper in small amounts; if it becomes too thick, stir in a splash of broth, or simmer uncovered briefly if it remains too thin.
  10. Step 10.Serve each Salisbury steak hot with plenty of mushroom-onion gravy, ideally alongside mashed potatoes, buttered noodles, green beans, or another simple vegetable.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Mix the beef gently to keep the patties tender.
  • Use the browned fond in the pan to deepen the gravy flavor.
  • Simmer rather than boil once the patties return to the gravy.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overworking the beef can make the patties dense.
  • Crowding the skillet prevents proper browning.
  • Adding broth too quickly can create lumps in the gravy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use button mushrooms instead of cremini.
  • Use crushed crackers instead of panko if necessary.
  • Use unsalted beef stock in place of low-sodium broth.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate patties and gravy together for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat gently over low heat until steaming hot.
  • Add a small splash of broth when reheating if the gravy has thickened.

Hot Cross Buns

Soft spiced hot cross buns studded with fruit, finished with flour crosses and glossy glaze.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 150 min🍽 12 servings🔥 215 cal
BreakfastAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Preppy Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 2 teaspoons instant yeast
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 teaspoons mixed spice
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 60 g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup sultanas
  • flour paste for crosses
  • apricot jam

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Combine flour, yeast, sugar and spice, then add warm milk, melted butter and egg to make a soft dough.
  2. Step 2.Knead until smooth and elastic, then fold through sultanas and leave covered until doubled in size.
  3. Step 3.Divide the risen dough into equal balls, arrange closely in a lined pan and let them rise again.
  4. Step 4.Pipe flour-and-water paste in crosses over each bun just before baking.
  5. Step 5.Bake until deeply golden and the buns sound hollow when tapped on their bases.
  6. Step 6.Warm apricot jam with a little water and brush it over hot buns for a glossy finish.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use warm, not hot, milk for the yeast.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding fruit before the dough develops can tear it.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use chopped dried apricots with raisins.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Freeze cooled buns up to one month.

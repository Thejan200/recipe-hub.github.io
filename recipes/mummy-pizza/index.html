Mummy Pizza

A crisp personal pizza covered with mozzarella bandages and olive eyes for an easy Halloween meal.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Use low-moisture mozzarella for clear strips. A common setback is Heavy sauce makes the pita soft.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 20 min🍽 2 servings🔥 340 cal
DinnerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 pita breads
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella strips
  • 8 olive slices
  • Italian seasoning

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 220°C and place the pitas on a baking sheet.
  2. Step 2.Spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving a narrow crust edge.
  3. Step 3.Lay mozzarella in loose uneven strips so the red sauce shows between the bandages.
  4. Step 4.Add olive slices in a small uncovered face space, then season lightly.
  5. Step 5.Bake until the pita is crisp and the cheese bubbles, then cool briefly before slicing.

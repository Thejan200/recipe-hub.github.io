Monster Hand Ham and Cheese Board

An easy Halloween grazing board that turns ham, cheese, crackers and fresh produce into a friendly monster-hand display.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Build the hand first, then fill the empty areas around it. To avoid a common problem, remember: Adding crackers underneath the ham makes the hand outline hard to see.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 25 min🍽 10 servings🔥 220 cal

Ingredients

  • 200 g sliced ham
  • 250 g mixed cheese cubes
  • 2 cups assorted crackers
  • 1 cup green grapes
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 carrots, cut into sticks
  • 5 blanched almonds
  • olives and mustard for details

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Choose a large clean board and place a small bowl of olives or mustard near one corner so the board has an anchor before the hand shape is built.
  2. Step 2.Fold the ham slices into loose ribbons and arrange them in a palm shape, keeping the centre generous enough to hold cheese cubes.
  3. Step 3.Build five fingers from alternating ham folds and cheese cubes, making the thumb shorter and set at a natural angle to the rest of the hand.
  4. Step 4.Press a blanched almond at each fingertip as a playful nail, using a tiny dab of mustard only if a piece will not stay in place.
  5. Step 5.Fill the centre of the palm with cheese cubes, then frame it with cucumber, carrots and grapes so the hand silhouette remains clear from above.
  6. Step 6.Fan crackers around the open areas rather than covering the hand, and tuck a few olives into gaps for contrast and easy snacking.
  7. Step 7.Check that every ingredient can be reached without moving the hand shape, then add serving tongs or small forks for a clean shared board.
  8. Step 8.Keep chilled until serving time and refresh any cut vegetables with a paper towel if moisture begins to collect on the board.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Build the hand first, then fill the empty areas around it.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding crackers underneath the ham makes the hand outline hard to see.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use turkey or roasted vegetables instead of ham.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cover and refrigerate components separately for up to one day before assembly.

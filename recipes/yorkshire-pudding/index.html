Yorkshire Pudding

Tall, crisp Yorkshire puddings made from eggs, flour, milk, salt, and hot fat for a classic roast dinner side.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 40 min🍽 12 servings🔥 112 cal
AppetizerUKChristmas

Watch the tutorial

Video: Preppy Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 120 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300 ml whole milk
  • 3 large eggs
  • 60 ml beef drippings, lard, or vegetable oil

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Whisk the flour and salt in one bowl. Whisk the eggs and whole milk in another, then gradually combine into a smooth batter. Refrigerate while the oven heats.
  2. Step 2.Heat the oven to 220°C. Add about one teaspoon of drippings or oil to each cup of a 12-cup metal muffin tin and heat the tin for about 10 minutes.
  3. Step 3.Stir the cold batter, then swiftly pour roughly 60 ml into each hot cup. Return the tin to the oven immediately.
  4. Step 4.Bake for about 20 minutes without opening the oven until tall, crisp and golden. Serve at once.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Rest the batter for a lighter texture.
  • Heat the fat until very hot before pouring batter.
  • Do not open the oven during the first rise.

! Common Mistakes

  • Putting batter into cool oil gives flat puddings.
  • Opening the oven door early lets steam escape.
  • Overfilling each tin cup stops the center setting.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use vegetable oil instead of beef dripping.
  • Use semi-skimmed milk if needed.
  • Bake in a muffin tin for individual puddings.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Best served as soon as baked.
  • Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days and re-crisp in a hot oven.

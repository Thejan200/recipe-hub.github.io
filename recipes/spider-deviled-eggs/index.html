Spider Deviled Eggs

Creamy deviled eggs topped with olive spiders for a classic, protein-rich Halloween appetizer.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Use a sharp paring knife to cut neat olive legs. A common setback is Warm eggs make the filling loose.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 32 min🍽 12 servings🔥 80 cal
AppetizerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 6 black olives
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Hard-cook the eggs, cool in ice water and peel them carefully.
  2. Step 2.Halve each egg and mash yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  3. Step 3.Spoon or pipe the filling into the egg whites.
  4. Step 4.Cut each olive into a body and thin legs, arranging one spider over each filled egg.
  5. Step 5.Keep chilled until serving and refrigerate promptly after the buffet.

Bubble and Squeak

Crisp-edged mashed potato and cabbage cakes fried in butter until golden outside and soft within.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 35 min🍽 4 servings🔥 290 cal

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked potatoes, mashed or crushed
  • 250 g cooked cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 large egg, optional for binding

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use cold cooked potato for better shaping.
  • Press cakes firmly before frying.
  • Let the underside brown before turning.

! Common Mistakes

  • Turning too early breaks the cakes.
  • Wet cabbage makes the mixture loose.
  • Crowding the pan prevents crisp edges.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use Brussels sprouts instead of cabbage.
  • Use leftover mashed potato if not too loose.
  • Omit the egg when the mixture holds together.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooked cakes for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat in a skillet for crispness.
  • Freeze separated by parchment for up to 1 month.

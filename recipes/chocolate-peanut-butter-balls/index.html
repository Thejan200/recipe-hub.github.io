Chocolate Peanut Butter Balls

Creamy peanut butter centres dipped in dark chocolate for an easy, crowd-pleasing holiday treat with a little flaky salt on top.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Chill the rolled centres before dipping. To avoid a common problem, remember: Overheating chocolate makes the coating thick and grainy.

Published 2026-11-11

⏱ 55 min🍽 24 servings🔥 175 cal
DessertUSAChristmas
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 10 ounces dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil
  • Flaky salt, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Line a small tray with parchment and stir the peanut butter, softened butter, powdered sugar and vanilla until the mixture is smooth enough to roll.
  2. Step 2.Roll the mixture into walnut-size balls and chill them for at least 25 minutes; a firm centre is the easiest way to get a clean chocolate coating.
  3. Step 3.Melt the chocolate with coconut oil in short microwave bursts, stirring between each burst until glossy; do not let steam or water touch the bowl.
  4. Step 4.Lower one chilled ball into the chocolate with a fork, lift it out and tap the fork against the bowl so excess chocolate falls back rather than pooling underneath.
  5. Step 5.Return each coated ball to the tray, add a tiny pinch of flaky salt if using, and chill until the shell is firm and snappy.
  6. Step 6.Serve slightly cool or pack between parchment layers; the centres taste especially creamy after five minutes at room temperature.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Chill the rolled centres before dipping.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overheating chocolate makes the coating thick and grainy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use semisweet chocolate for a sweeter shell.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate airtight for up to 1 week.

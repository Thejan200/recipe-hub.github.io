Slow Cooker Lamb

Tender slow-cooker Australian lamb leg with rosemary, garlic and rich pan juices.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 440 min🍽 6 servings🔥 510 cal
DinnerAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: RecipeTin Eats

Ingredients

  • 1.8 kg lamb leg
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • potatoes and carrots

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Season the lamb generously and rub it with olive oil, crushed garlic and chopped rosemary.
  2. Step 2.Place sliced onion, potatoes and carrots in the slow cooker to form a flavourful bed for the lamb.
  3. Step 3.Set the lamb on top, then add stock and Worcestershire sauce around, not over, the seasoned surface.
  4. Step 4.Cook on low until the lamb is fork-tender and pulls away easily without needing to be carved forcefully.
  5. Step 5.Rest lamb under foil while you strain and reduce the cooking juices if a thicker gravy is desired.
  6. Step 6.Slice or shred the lamb and serve with the vegetables and hot rosemary gravy.

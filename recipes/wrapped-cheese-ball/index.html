Wrapped Halloween Cheese Ball

A creamy herb cheese ball wrapped in cucumber ribbons for a fresh, playful Halloween party centrepiece.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Dry the cucumber thoroughly before decorating. Keep this in mind too: Warm cream cheese loses the rounded shape.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 1 hr 20 min🍽 12 servings🔥 190 cal

Ingredients

  • 450 g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 tablespoons chopped chives
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 long cucumber
  • 2 olive slices
  • crackers and vegetables for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Beat the softened cream cheese, cheddar, chives, lemon juice and garlic powder until the mixture is evenly seasoned and can hold a rounded shape.
  2. Step 2.Scrape the cheese mixture onto a sheet of baking paper and use clean hands to form a tight ball with no cracks around the sides.
  3. Step 3.Chill the ball for at least one hour so the surface firms enough to support the cucumber decoration without flattening.
  4. Step 4.Use a vegetable peeler to make long thin cucumber ribbons, then pat them dry so moisture does not make the cheese ball slippery.
  5. Step 5.Lay the ribbons across the chilled ball in criss-crossing bands, leaving narrow gaps so the pale cheese still reads as a mummy-style wrap.
  6. Step 6.Add olive slices near the front as eyes, then move the finished ball carefully to a platter with crackers and crisp vegetable sticks.
  7. Step 7.Keep the platter chilled until close to serving time, because the cheese softens quickly in a warm party room.
  8. Step 8.Use a small spreading knife for the cheese ball and replace it with a clean utensil if it touches crackers or other shared food.

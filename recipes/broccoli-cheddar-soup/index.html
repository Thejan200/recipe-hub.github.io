Broccoli Cheddar Soup

A rich broccoli and cheddar soup with small tender florets, grated carrot, and a smooth cheese base that stays spoonable rather than overly thick.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Shred a block of cheddar shortly before cooking for the smoothest melt. Keep this in mind too: High heat after adding cheddar can cause the fat and proteins to separate.

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup half-and-half
  • 4 cups small broccoli florets
  • 1 large carrot, grated
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups freshly shredded sharp cheddar

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Melt butter in a heavy soup pot over medium heat and cook the finely diced onion until soft, taking care not to brown the butter.
  2. Step 2.Whisk the flour into the onion and cook the paste for 1 to 2 minutes until it smells lightly toasted rather than raw.
  3. Step 3.Gradually whisk in the vegetable broth, followed by the milk and half-and-half, until the liquid is completely smooth.
  4. Step 4.Add broccoli, carrot, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, then bring the soup only to a quiet simmer.
  5. Step 5.Partly cover and cook over medium-low heat until the broccoli stems are tender but the florets have not disappeared, about 15 minutes.
  6. Step 6.Press a small portion of the cooked broccoli against the side of the pot if a thicker soup is wanted, leaving plenty of pieces whole.
  7. Step 7.Lower the heat as far as possible and stir in the cheddar a handful at a time, waiting for each addition to melt before adding more.
  8. Step 8.Check the seasoning and serve while hot, without bringing the cheese soup back to a boil.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Shred a block of cheddar shortly before cooking for the smoothest melt.
  • Cut large florets in half so the broccoli fits comfortably on a spoon.
  • Keep the pot at low heat once the cheese goes in to protect the creamy texture.

! Common Mistakes

  • High heat after adding cheddar can cause the fat and proteins to separate.
  • Pouring the broth in too quickly makes it harder to whisk out flour lumps.
  • Leaving broccoli stems in large chunks may leave them firm when the florets are ready.

↔ Substitutions

  • Chicken broth can be used when a vegetarian soup is not needed.
  • Mild cheddar gives a softer cheese flavour than sharp cheddar.
  • Use more whole milk in place of half-and-half for a less rich bowl.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat slowly on the stovetop, stirring regularly and stopping before it boils.
  • Freezing is not ideal because the dairy and cheese base may separate after thawing.

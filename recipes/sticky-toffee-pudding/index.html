Sticky Toffee Pudding

Soft date sponge served warm with a glossy butter, brown sugar, and cream toffee sauce.

⏱ 60 min🍽 8 servings🔥 560 cal

Ingredients

  • 225 g pitted dates, chopped
  • 240 ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 75 g softened butter
  • 150 g dark brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 175 g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 100 g butter for sauce
  • 150 g dark brown sugar for sauce
  • 180 ml double cream
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Soften dates fully before blending.
  • Do not overmix after adding flour.
  • Serve sauce warm for the classic finish.

! Common Mistakes

  • Large date pieces make uneven sponge.
  • Overbaking dries the pudding.
  • Boiling sauce too long makes it grainy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use heavy cream for double cream.
  • Use molasses only if golden syrup is unavailable.
  • Bake as individual puddings with reduced time.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate sponge and sauce for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat gently before serving.
  • Freeze sponge without sauce for up to 2 months.

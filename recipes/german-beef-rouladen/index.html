German Beef Rouladen

Thin beef rolls with mustard, bacon, onion, and pickle, braised in a deep savory gravy.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Keep filling pieces slim so every roll closes tightly. A common setback is Skipping the browning step leaves the gravy pale and less savory.

Ingredients

  • 4 thin top-round beef slices
  • 4 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • 4 bacon slices
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 dill pickles, cut into spears
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Lay the beef slices flat and season them lightly, then spread a thin layer of mustard over each piece while leaving a small border at the edges.
  2. Step 2.Set bacon, a few onion strands, and a pickle spear near one short edge of each slice, using a modest amount so the filling does not force the rolls open.
  3. Step 3.Roll the beef tightly around the filling and secure the seam with kitchen twine or toothpicks, checking that the pickle stays centred as the roll closes.
  4. Step 4.Brown the rouladen in hot oil in a Dutch oven, turning carefully until every side has color, then transfer the rolls briefly to a plate.
  5. Step 5.Stir tomato paste into the pot, add flour, and slowly pour in stock while scraping the browned bits from the bottom to build the gravy base.
  6. Step 6.Return the rolls to the pot, cover, and braise gently in the oven until the beef is tender enough to cut easily with a fork.
  7. Step 7.Remove the twine or toothpicks, then simmer the gravy uncovered if it needs to reduce; season only after tasting because bacon and stock add salt.
  8. Step 8.Serve each roulade with gravy, red cabbage, and potatoes or dumplings, cutting one open at the table to show the spiral filling.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep filling pieces slim so every roll closes tightly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the browning step leaves the gravy pale and less savory.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use turkey bacon if preferred, but season the rolls more generously.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate in the gravy for up to 3 days and warm slowly on the stove.

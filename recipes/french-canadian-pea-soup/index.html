French Canadian Pea Soup

Traditional French Canadian yellow pea soup with ham.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 4 hr 50 min🍽 8 servings🔥 310 cal
SoupCanadaPorkDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: BBQ Joes

Ingredients

  • 1 pound dried yellow peas
  • 1/2 pound salt pork or ham
  • 3 carrots, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 10 cups water
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Rinse the peas and check them for debris.
  2. Step 2.Brown the salt pork in a large soup pot.
  3. Step 3.Cook carrots and onion in the rendered fat until softened.
  4. Step 4.Stir in peas, bay leaves, parsley and water.
  5. Step 5.Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer.
  6. Step 6.Cook partly covered until the peas are tender and thick.
  7. Step 7.Remove bay leaves and adjust salt and pepper.
  8. Step 8.Rest for ten minutes before ladling into bowls.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Rinse and sort dried peas before cooking to remove any small debris.
  • Keep the soup at a steady gentle simmer and stir occasionally so peas do not catch on the pot bottom.
  • Mash only part of the peas if you prefer a thicker soup with some visible texture.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding salt too early when using salty ham, which can make the finished soup overly salty.
  • Stopping the simmer before the split peas are fully tender.
  • Adding too little liquid as the peas thicken during cooking.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a ham hock, leftover ham, or omit ham for a vegetarian version with vegetable stock.
  • Use yellow or green split peas, noting that colour and flavour will vary slightly.
  • Use celery leaves or parsley as a finishing herb if fresh thyme is unavailable.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
  • The soup thickens in the fridge; add water or stock while reheating if needed.
  • Freeze cooled portions in freezer-safe containers for up to 3 months.

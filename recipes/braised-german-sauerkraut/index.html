Braised German Sauerkraut

Gently braised sauerkraut with apple, onion, and caraway for a mellow, savory German-style side.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Taste before seasoning because sauerkraut brands vary widely in saltiness. Keep this in mind too: Rinsing all the sauerkraut flavor away leaves the dish flat.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 tart apple, grated
  • 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
  • 1/2 cup apple juice
  • 1 bay leaf
  • black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Drain the sauerkraut, tasting a small strand first; rinse it briefly only if it is much saltier or sharper than you want in the finished side dish.
  2. Step 2.Melt butter in a medium saucepan and cook the onion over medium-low heat until soft and sweet, keeping it pale rather than browned.
  3. Step 3.Add the sauerkraut, grated apple, caraway, apple juice, and bay leaf, then toss thoroughly so the apple and seasonings are spread through the cabbage.
  4. Step 4.Cover the pan and simmer gently, stirring occasionally, until the liquid reduces and the sauerkraut tastes mellow while still retaining a little texture.
  5. Step 5.Uncover for the last few minutes if excess liquid remains, letting the cabbage cook down without allowing the bottom of the pan to scorch.
  6. Step 6.Remove the bay leaf and season with black pepper after tasting; most jarred sauerkraut brings enough salt of its own.
  7. Step 7.For a richer version, stir in a small spoon of butter just before serving so it melts through the warm cabbage.
  8. Step 8.Serve alongside bratwurst, pork roast, or potato dumplings, keeping leftovers for another hearty meal.

! Common Mistakes

↔ Substitutions

  • Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 5 days and reheat gently.

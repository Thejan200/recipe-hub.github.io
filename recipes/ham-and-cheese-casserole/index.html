Ham and Cheese Casserole

A ham-and-cheese pasta bake with a stovetop cheese sauce, a short oven finish, and a crunchy Parmesan crumb topping.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Leave the pasta slightly firm before baking so it keeps its shape. To avoid a common problem, remember: Pasta cooked completely before baking can become too soft.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz short pasta such as rotini or shells
  • 3 cups diced cooked ham
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 cups sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
  • 1 cup Gruyere or Monterey Jack, shredded
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 375°F, grease a 9-by-13-inch baking dish, and bring a pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Step 2.Cook the pasta just short of al dente and drain it well; it will continue cooking in the oven.
  3. Step 3.Melt two tablespoons of butter in a saucepan and soften the onion over medium heat.
  4. Step 4.Stir in flour for about a minute, then whisk in the milk gradually until the sauce base is smooth.
  5. Step 5.Let it thicken gently, then add Dijon, pepper, cheddar, and Gruyere over low heat so the cheese melts without boiling.
  6. Step 6.Fold in the ham and pasta, tasting before salting because both main ingredients are already seasoned.
  7. Step 7.Spread the mixture through the baking dish without packing it down.
  8. Step 8.Combine the remaining melted butter with panko and Parmesan and scatter it evenly over the surface.
  9. Step 9.Bake uncovered until the edges bubble and the crumbs turn golden.
  10. Step 10.Rest several minutes before serving so the sauce settles.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Leave the pasta slightly firm before baking so it keeps its shape.
  • Grate cheese from a block when possible for a smoother sauce.
  • Season last, after the ham and cheese have been mixed through.

! Common Mistakes

  • Pasta cooked completely before baking can become too soft.
  • A vigorously boiling cheese sauce may lose its smooth texture.
  • Pre-shredded cheese can give a less even melt.

↔ Substitutions

  • Monterey Jack works in place of Gruyere.
  • Use shells, penne, or elbow macaroni in place of rotini.
  • Stir in peas or lightly steamed broccoli for a vegetable addition.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate covered leftovers for up to four days.
  • Reheat a covered portion until hot, adding a little milk if it seems dry.
  • Freeze cooled baked portions for up to two months.

Ham and Cheese Casserole

Creamy baked pasta folded with smoky ham and two cheeses, finished with a crisp buttery breadcrumb topping.

⏱ 55 min🍽 8 servings🔥 570 cal

Ingredients

  • 12 oz short pasta such as rotini or shells
  • 3 cups diced cooked ham
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 cups sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
  • 1 cup Gruyere or Monterey Jack, shredded
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 375°F, lightly grease a 9-by-13-inch baking dish, and bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
  2. Step 2.Cook the pasta for about 2 minutes less than the package's al dente time, then drain well so it does not become overly soft in the oven.
  3. Step 3.Melt two tablespoons of butter in a saucepan over medium heat, add the onion, and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Step 4.Sprinkle in the flour and stir constantly for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in the milk until the mixture is smooth with no pockets of dry flour.
  5. Step 5.Simmer gently for 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce thickens, then stir in Dijon, black pepper, cheddar, and Gruyere until melted and smooth.
  6. Step 6.Fold the drained pasta and diced ham into the cheese sauce, tasting before adding any extra salt because both ham and cheese are naturally salty.
  7. Step 7.Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish and spread it into an even layer without pressing the pasta down tightly.
  8. Step 8.Melt the remaining tablespoon of butter and combine it with panko and Parmesan, then scatter the crumbs evenly over the casserole.
  9. Step 9.Bake uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes until the edges bubble and the breadcrumb topping is golden brown.
  10. Step 10.Rest for 5 to 10 minutes before serving so the sauce settles slightly and each portion can be spooned out cleanly.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Undercook pasta slightly before baking.
  • Shred cheese from a block for a smoother sauce.
  • Taste before salting because ham and cheese are already salty.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overcooked pasta becomes soft after baking.
  • Boiling the cheese sauce can make it grainy.
  • Pre-shredded cheese may melt less smoothly.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use Monterey Jack instead of Gruyere.
  • Use shells, penne, or elbow macaroni in place of rotini.
  • Add frozen peas or lightly steamed broccoli for vegetables.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate covered casserole for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat portions covered until hot, adding a splash of milk if dry.
  • Freeze baked casserole portions for up to 2 months.

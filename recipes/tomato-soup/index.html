Tomato Soup

A smooth tomato and basil soup built from canned whole tomatoes, gently softened vegetables, and vegetable broth for a comforting lunch or light supper.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 3 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tsp sugar, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Warm the olive oil in a soup pot over medium heat, then add the onion, carrots, and celery with a small pinch of salt.
  2. Step 2.Cook, stirring now and then, until the vegetables are softened and the onion looks translucent, about 8 minutes.
  3. Step 3.Add the garlic and tomato paste. Stir for about a minute so the paste cooks onto the vegetables without letting the garlic colour too darkly.
  4. Step 4.Pour in the tomatoes and all their juices, breaking the tomatoes apart with a spoon as they go into the pot.
  5. Step 5.Add the vegetable broth, oregano, salt, and pepper, and bring the pot to a calm simmer while scraping any flavourful bits from the base.
  6. Step 6.Partly cover and cook gently for roughly 25 minutes, until the carrots are very tender and the tomato flavour no longer tastes raw.
  7. Step 7.Take the pot off the heat, add the basil, and purée with an immersion blender, keeping the blade below the surface throughout.
  8. Step 8.Taste the soup after blending; use the optional sugar only if the tomatoes taste overly sharp, then adjust salt and pepper.
  9. Step 9.Warm briefly if needed and serve with toast, crackers, or a grilled cheese sandwich.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Whole canned tomatoes give the soup a dependable base when fresh tomatoes are out of season.
  • If using a countertop blender, let the soup cool slightly and blend small batches with the lid vented safely.
  • Season after puréeing, because blending distributes the salt and herbs more evenly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Letting garlic scorch can add an unpleasant bitter note to an otherwise mild soup.
  • A hard boil can cook off too much liquid before the vegetables are fully soft.
  • Sealing hot soup in a full blender can build pressure and cause splashes.

↔ Substitutions

  • Chicken broth may be used in place of vegetable broth when a vegetarian soup is not required.
  • Use 1 teaspoon dried basil if fresh basil is unavailable, adding it with the oregano.
  • Fire-roasted canned tomatoes give the finished soup a subtle charred flavour.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool the soup promptly and refrigerate it in a covered container for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat over low to medium heat, stirring occasionally until hot throughout.
  • Freeze in individual portions for up to 3 months and defrost in the refrigerator overnight.

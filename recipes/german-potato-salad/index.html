Warm German Potato Salad

Tender potatoes tossed warm with a bright mustard-vinegar dressing, onion, and fresh herbs.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Dress the potatoes while warm for the best flavor absorption. A common setback is Mixing too aggressively breaks warm potatoes into a paste.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb small waxy potatoes
  • 4 slices bacon, diced
  • 1 small red onion, finely diced
  • 1/3 cup chicken or vegetable stock
  • 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoons chopped chives
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Simmer the potatoes in salted water until a knife enters with little resistance, then drain and let them cool only until they can be handled comfortably.
  2. Step 2.Slice the potatoes while still warm so their surfaces are ready to absorb the dressing rather than repelling it after they become fully cold.
  3. Step 3.Cook the bacon in a skillet until crisp, then transfer it to a plate while leaving a small amount of the rendered fat in the pan.
  4. Step 4.Add onion to the warm bacon fat and cook just until softened, avoiding a hard brown color that would overpower the light dressing.
  5. Step 5.Pour in stock, vinegar, mustard, and sugar, whisking until the mixture comes together and begins to steam gently.
  6. Step 6.Pour the hot dressing over the sliced potatoes and fold with a broad spoon, taking care not to break the pieces into mash.
  7. Step 7.Let the salad stand for ten minutes, then taste and adjust salt, pepper, or vinegar once the potatoes have had time to absorb the seasoning.
  8. Step 8.Fold in bacon and chives just before serving warm or at room temperature.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Dress the potatoes while warm for the best flavor absorption.

! Common Mistakes

  • Mixing too aggressively breaks warm potatoes into a paste.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use vegetable stock and omit bacon for a vegetarian version.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 3 days; bring toward room temperature before serving.

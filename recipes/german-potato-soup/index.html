Creamy German Potato Soup

A creamy potato and vegetable soup with leeks, carrots, herbs, and a comforting German home-cooking feel.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Blend only part of the soup to keep it both creamy and textured. To avoid a common problem, remember: Boiling after dairy is added can make the soup look grainy.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 leek, sliced and rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, diced
  • 5 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon dried marjoram
  • 1/2 cup milk or cream
  • salt, pepper, and chopped parsley

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Melt butter in a soup pot and cook leek, onion, and carrot with a pinch of salt until the vegetables soften and smell sweet rather than raw.
  2. Step 2.Add diced potatoes, stock, bay leaf, and marjoram, then bring the pot just to a boil before lowering the heat to a quiet simmer.
  3. Step 3.Cook until the potatoes break easily when pressed with a spoon, checking the pot occasionally and adding a splash of water only if the soup becomes too thick too early.
  4. Step 4.Remove the bay leaf and blend part of the soup with an immersion blender, leaving some potato and vegetable pieces intact for a more satisfying texture.
  5. Step 5.Stir in milk or cream over low heat and warm gently without boiling hard, which keeps the soup smooth and prevents dairy from separating.
  6. Step 6.Taste and season with salt and pepper, remembering that stock varieties can begin at very different salt levels.
  7. Step 7.If the soup is thicker than you prefer, loosen it with warm stock; if thinner, simmer uncovered for a few minutes before serving.
  8. Step 8.Ladle into bowls and finish with parsley, rye bread, or a spoon of sour cream if desired.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Blend only part of the soup to keep it both creamy and textured.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling after dairy is added can make the soup look grainy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use oat cream and dairy-free butter for a plant-based version.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 4 days; loosen with stock while reheating.

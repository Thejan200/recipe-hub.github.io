Prawn Cocktail

Classic chilled prawn cocktail with crisp lettuce, lemon and a creamy pink seafood sauce.

Published 2026-10-21

⏱ 20 min🍽 4 servings🔥 280 cal
AppetizerAustraliaSeafood
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Pinch of Mint

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked peeled prawns
  • 1 little gem lettuce or romaine heart
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • pinch of paprika
  • 1 avocado, optional
  • lemon wedges

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Check the cooked prawns carefully for any remaining shell fragments, then chill them until the moment you are ready to assemble the cocktails.
  2. Step 2.Whisk mayonnaise, tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice and paprika until the dressing is smooth, pink and evenly seasoned.
  3. Step 3.Taste the sauce and add a small extra squeeze of lemon only if needed, keeping the flavour creamy rather than sharp or watery.
  4. Step 4.Wash and dry the lettuce thoroughly, then shred it finely so the base of each serving remains crisp instead of diluting the sauce.
  5. Step 5.If using avocado, cut it shortly before serving and toss it with a tiny amount of lemon juice to protect its colour.
  6. Step 6.Spoon lettuce into chilled glasses or bowls, then arrange the prawns around and above the lettuce so they stay visible and appealing.
  7. Step 7.Add avocado if using, spoon the pink sauce over or alongside the prawns, and dust very lightly with paprika for colour.
  8. Step 8.Serve immediately with lemon wedges and small spoons, keeping the remaining prawns and sauce refrigerated until required.

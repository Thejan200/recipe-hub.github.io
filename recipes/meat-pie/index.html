Beef · Medium

Meat Pie

Classic Australian-style individual meat pies with a tender minced-beef filling and flaky pastry.

Published 2026-10-21

⏱ 1 hr🍽 4 servings🔥 640 cal
BeefAustraliaDinner

Video: My World Kitchen Official

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 tablespoon plain flour
  • 1 tablespoon tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Vegemite, optional
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • shortcrust and puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Cook the onion in oil over medium heat until soft and translucent, taking care not to brown it too quickly or leave it crunchy.
  2. Step 2.Add beef mince and cook while breaking it apart thoroughly, then drain only any clearly excessive fat from the pan.
  3. Step 3.Sprinkle flour over the mince and stir for one minute so the flour coats the meat and loses its raw taste before liquid is added.
  4. Step 4.Add tomato sauce, optional Vegemite and beef stock, then simmer until the mixture is glossy, thick and spoonable rather than watery.
  5. Step 5.Cool the filling fully while you line individual pie tins with shortcrust pastry, pressing it gently into the corners without stretching it.
  6. Step 6.Spoon the cooled filling into the pastry cases, top with puff pastry, press the edges together and trim away the excess neatly.
  7. Step 7.Brush the lids with beaten egg and cut a small slit in each top so steam can escape without splitting the pastry.
  8. Step 8.Bake until the pastry is puffed and richly golden, then rest briefly and serve with tomato sauce if you enjoy it.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • A very thick filling gives the neatest Australian-style pie slice.

! Common Mistakes

  • Filling the pies while the mince mixture is still hot.

↔ Substitutions

  • Leave out Vegemite and add a little extra Worcestershire sauce for savouriness.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep refrigerated for up to two days and reheat until piping hot.

German Bratwurst with Sauerkraut

Pan-seared bratwurst with gently braised sauerkraut, mustard, and a soft pretzel on the side.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Brown bratwurst after cooking it gently so the casings stay intact. One thing to watch for is Boiling sausages hard can make their skins burst and lose moisture.

Ingredients

  • 4 uncooked bratwurst sausages
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 cups sauerkraut, drained
  • 1/2 cup apple juice
  • 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • coarse mustard for serving
  • 4 soft pretzels or rolls

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Warm the oil in a covered skillet over medium-low heat and cook the bratwurst slowly at first so the centres heat without the skins splitting.
  2. Step 2.Turn the sausages every few minutes, then uncover and raise the heat near the end to brown all sides with an even, appetizing color.
  3. Step 3.Transfer the browned bratwurst to a plate and add butter and sliced onion to the same pan, scraping up the browned bits left behind.
  4. Step 4.Cook the onion until soft, then add drained sauerkraut, apple juice, caraway, and Dijon mustard and stir everything together.
  5. Step 5.Cover and simmer the sauerkraut gently until the liquid has mostly cooked away and the cabbage tastes warm, mellow, and seasoned rather than sharply raw.
  6. Step 6.Return the bratwurst to the skillet for a few minutes so they warm through with the sauerkraut, keeping the pan at a quiet simmer.
  7. Step 7.Check that uncooked sausages have reached 160°F in the centre before serving, then taste the sauerkraut and add pepper or a little mustard if wanted.
  8. Step 8.Pile the sauerkraut onto plates, set a bratwurst alongside, and serve with coarse mustard and a warm pretzel or bread roll.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Brown bratwurst after cooking it gently so the casings stay intact.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling sausages hard can make their skins burst and lose moisture.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use alcohol-free apple cider in place of apple juice for a tangier braise.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days and reheat gently in a covered skillet.

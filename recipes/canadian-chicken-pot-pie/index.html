Chicken Pot Pie

Creamy chicken and vegetable filling beneath a flaky golden crust.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 1 hr 10 min🍽 8 servings🔥 520 cal
ChickenCanadaDinner

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, diced
  • 1 cup carrots, diced
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1/2 cup celery, diced
  • 1/2 cup onion, diced
  • 1/3 cup butter
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1 3/4 cups chicken broth
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 pie crusts
  • salt, pepper and thyme

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Step 2.Cook onion, celery and carrots in butter until tender.
  3. Step 3.Stir in flour and cook for one minute.
  4. Step 4.Whisk in broth and milk until the sauce thickens.
  5. Step 5.Fold in chicken, peas and seasoning.
  6. Step 6.Line a pie dish with one crust and add the cooled filling.
  7. Step 7.Cover with the second crust, seal the edge and cut vents.
  8. Step 8.Bake until deeply golden and bubbling, then rest before slicing.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cook the filling until it is thick enough to coat a spoon before it goes under the pastry.
  • Cut chicken and vegetables into bite-size pieces so servings are easy to eat.
  • Vent the pastry top so steam can escape and the crust stays crisp.

! Common Mistakes

  • Using a filling that is too thin, which can make the bottom of the pastry soggy.
  • Putting cold pastry over a very hot filling without working promptly, which can soften it.
  • Overfilling the dish and allowing sauce to spill over during baking.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use leftover roast chicken or turkey in place of freshly cooked chicken.
  • Use a mix of peas, carrots, corn, or green beans based on what you have.
  • Use individual oven-safe dishes for smaller pies, adjusting baking time as needed.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled pie for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat uncovered in the oven so the pastry can crisp again.
  • Freeze the assembled unbaked pie when practical; thaw in the refrigerator before baking.

Creepy Deviled Egg Eyeballs

Classic deviled eggs dressed with olive pupils and fine pepper details for an easy Halloween platter.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Older eggs usually peel more cleanly than very fresh eggs. One thing to watch for is Overcooking makes yolks dry and difficult to mash.

⏱ 32 min🍽 12 servings🔥 82 cal

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon pickle brine
  • salt and pepper
  • 6 black olive slices
  • thin strips of roasted red pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Place the eggs in a saucepan, cover with cold water, bring just to a boil, then cover and remove from the heat for a gentle, even cook.
  2. Step 2.After twelve minutes, transfer the eggs to ice water so the shells loosen and the yolks stay bright rather than developing a grey ring.
  3. Step 3.Peel the cooled eggs, halve them lengthwise and carefully lift the yolks into a bowl while keeping the whites intact for the platter.
  4. Step 4.Mash the yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon, pickle brine, salt and pepper until smooth enough to pipe but not loose enough to run.
  5. Step 5.Pipe or spoon the filling into each white half, building a small rounded mound that gives the olive eye decoration a stable base.
  6. Step 6.Set an olive slice on each mound as a pupil, then add a few delicate roasted-pepper strips around it for a playful eye detail.
  7. Step 7.Arrange the eggs on a chilled plate so they cannot slide, keeping the decorated sides facing upward for the strongest visual effect.
  8. Step 8.Serve cold and return the platter to refrigeration if it sits out for more than two hours.

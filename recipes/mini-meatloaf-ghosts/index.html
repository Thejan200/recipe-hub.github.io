Mini Meatloaf Ghosts

Tender individual meatloaves topped with piped potato ghosts for a warm Halloween dinner.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Warm potatoes pipe more smoothly than cold potatoes. A common setback is Overfilled cups spill fat around the potato topping.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 48 min🍽 12 servings🔥 210 cal
DinnerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 2 cups mashed potatoes
  • olive pieces
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and divide the seasoned meatloaf mixture into greased muffin cups.
  2. Step 2.Bake until each centre reaches 71°C, then let the portions rest while preparing smooth warm mashed potatoes.
  3. Step 3.Pipe the potatoes in tall swirls over each meatloaf to create a ghost shape.
  4. Step 4.Press two tiny olive pieces into every potato ghost as eyes.
  5. Step 5.Return to the oven for a few minutes only to warm the topping, then serve.

Pavlova

Classic Australian pavlova with a crisp meringue shell, marshmallow centre, cream and fresh fruit.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 1 hr 40 min🍽 8 servings🔥 245 cal
DessertAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Preppy Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 cup caster sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cornflour
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 cup thickened cream
  • fresh berries and kiwi

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 120°C and line a tray with baking paper, drawing a 20 cm circle as a guide.
  2. Step 2.Whip egg whites to soft peaks, then add sugar one spoonful at a time until the meringue is glossy and firm.
  3. Step 3.Fold through cornflour, vinegar and vanilla gently, keeping as much air in the meringue as possible.
  4. Step 4.Spoon the meringue into the circle, shape high sides and a shallow centre, then bake until crisp outside.
  5. Step 5.Turn the oven off and cool the pavlova inside with the door slightly ajar to reduce cracking.
  6. Step 6.Top the completely cool shell with softly whipped cream and fruit immediately before serving.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

! Common Mistakes

Shrimp Cocktail

Classic American shrimp cocktail with chilled poached shrimp and a tangy homemade cocktail sauce.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 20 min🍽 6 servings🔥 185 cal

Ingredients

  • 2 lb large raw shrimp, peeled and deveined, tails left on
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1/2 small onion, sliced
  • 1 cup ice
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup prepared horseradish
  • 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • hot sauce, to taste
  • lemon wedges, to serve

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Fill a large saucepan with the water, salt, lemon halves and sliced onion, then bring it to a gentle boil over medium-high heat so the poaching liquid is fragrant before the shrimp are added.
  2. Step 2.While the water heats, fill a large bowl with cold water and ice, leaving enough room for the shrimp to be fully submerged after cooking.
  3. Step 3.Add the raw shrimp to the gently boiling liquid in one batch, stirring once so they cook evenly without sticking together.
  4. Step 4.Cook only until the shrimp are pink, curled and opaque through the thickest part, usually about two to three minutes depending on their size.
  5. Step 5.Lift the shrimp out promptly with a slotted spoon and transfer them directly to the ice water so the cooking stops and the texture stays tender.
  6. Step 6.Whisk the ketchup, prepared horseradish, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce and a few drops of hot sauce in a small bowl until the cocktail sauce is smooth and balanced.
  7. Step 7.Taste the cocktail sauce and adjust the horseradish, lemon or hot sauce in small amounts, keeping it tangy and savoury rather than overly spicy.
  8. Step 8.Drain the chilled shrimp well and pat them dry if needed, then arrange them around a serving bowl of cocktail sauce with the tails facing outward.
  9. Step 9.Serve immediately with lemon wedges, or refrigerate the shrimp and sauce separately until ready to assemble and serve cold.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use an ice bath immediately after poaching so the shrimp stay tender and chilled instead of becoming rubbery.
  • Taste and adjust the cocktail sauce gradually because horseradish and hot sauce can vary in strength.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overcooking the shrimp after they turn opaque, which makes their texture tough.
  • Leaving the shrimp in the poaching water instead of transferring them straight to ice water.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use cooked chilled shrimp when needed, then prepare only the homemade cocktail sauce and assemble before serving.
  • Use lime juice instead of lemon juice for a slightly different citrus finish.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate the cooked shrimp and cocktail sauce in separate covered containers for up to two days.
  • Do not leave cooked shrimp at room temperature for more than two hours.

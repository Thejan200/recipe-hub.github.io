German Erbsensuppe

A thick, comforting German split pea soup with vegetables, marjoram, and optional smoked ham.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Stir split pea soup regularly near the end because it thickens quickly. A common setback is Adding salt early can make it difficult to balance a salty smoked ham.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried green split peas
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 small potato, diced
  • 6 cups vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon dried marjoram
  • 1 cup diced smoked ham (optional)
  • salt, pepper, and vinegar

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Rinse the split peas in a sieve and pick out any small stones or shrivelled pieces, then set them aside while the vegetable base is started.
  2. Step 2.Heat oil in a soup pot and cook onion, carrot, celery, and potato with a pinch of salt until the vegetables begin to soften around the edges.
  3. Step 3.Add peas, stock, bay leaf, and marjoram, stirring well so the peas do not settle in one thick layer on the bottom of the pot.
  4. Step 4.Bring to a gentle simmer and cook partially covered, stirring from time to time, until the peas soften and begin to thicken the broth naturally.
  5. Step 5.Add smoked ham during the final part of cooking if using, allowing it to warm through and lend a savory note without overwhelming the peas.
  6. Step 6.Remove the bay leaf and blend a small portion of the soup only if you want it smoother; leaving it rustic is equally appropriate.
  7. Step 7.Season with pepper and a small splash of vinegar, then taste before adding salt because stock and smoked ham can contribute plenty.
  8. Step 8.Serve hot with rye bread, adjusting the consistency with hot stock if the soup thickens as it stands.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Stir split pea soup regularly near the end because it thickens quickly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding salt early can make it difficult to balance a salty smoked ham.

↔ Substitutions

  • Leave out ham and use vegetable stock for a vegetarian soup.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 2 months.

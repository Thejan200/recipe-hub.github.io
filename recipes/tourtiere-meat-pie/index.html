Tourtière Meat Pie

French Canadian double-crust meat pie with warm spices.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 1 hr 40 min🍽 8 servings🔥 650 cal
PorkCanadaBeefDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Blue Jean Chef

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 pound ground pork
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 celery ribs, chopped
  • 28 ounces canned tomatoes, drained
  • 3/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 3/4 teaspoon dried savory
  • 1/2 cup fresh breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Make the pastry and chill it until firm.
  2. Step 2.Cook onion, celery and garlic in oil until softened.
  3. Step 3.Brown pork and beef, breaking it into small pieces.
  4. Step 4.Add tomatoes, cinnamon and savory and simmer until thick.
  5. Step 5.Stir in breadcrumbs and cool the filling completely.
  6. Step 6.Line a pie plate with rolled pastry and add the filling.
  7. Step 7.Top with the remaining pastry, seal, vent and egg-wash.
  8. Step 8.Bake at 375°F (190°C) until deeply browned, then rest before slicing.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cool the filling before adding it to the pastry so the bottom crust stays firmer.
  • Taste the filling before assembling and adjust seasoning while it is still easy to mix.
  • Use a small amount of liquid only if needed; tourtière filling should be moist, not soupy.

! Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the cooling step and softening the pastry with hot filling.
  • Leaving large chunks of meat or onion that make neat slices difficult.
  • Cutting the pie immediately after baking before the filling has settled.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a mix of pork and beef, or use all pork, according to preference.
  • Use prepared pastry if you need a quicker assembly, while keeping the filling homemade.
  • Serve with tomato chutney or fruit ketchup for a traditional-style sweet-tart contrast.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled pie for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat slices in the oven until hot so the pastry stays crisper than it would in a microwave.
  • Freeze baked slices well wrapped for up to 2 months.

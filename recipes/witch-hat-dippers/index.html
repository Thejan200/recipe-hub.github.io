Witch Hat Dippers

Crisp tortilla cones with seasoned cream-cheese filling, shaped as savory witch hats for dipping.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Assemble close to serving so the tortilla stays crisp. Keep this in mind too: Warm cones soften the cheese filling.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 28 min🍽 12 servings🔥 145 cal
AppetizerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 6 small flour tortillas
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 225 g cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated cheddar
  • 2 tablespoons chopped scallions
  • 1 teaspoon taco seasoning
  • salsa for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and brush both sides of tortilla wedges lightly with oil.
  2. Step 2.Bake wedges until crisp, then cool completely so they stay pointed and crunchy.
  3. Step 3.Mix cream cheese, cheddar, scallions and taco seasoning until spreadable.
  4. Step 4.Spoon the filling onto round cracker bases and stand a tortilla cone in the centre of each one.
  5. Step 5.Serve with salsa alongside so guests can dip without softening the hats in advance.

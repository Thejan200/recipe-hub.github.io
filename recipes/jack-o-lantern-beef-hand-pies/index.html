Jack-O-Lantern Beef Hand Pies

Flaky individual beef pies with carved pumpkin-style pastry tops for a portable Halloween meal.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Thick filling keeps the hand pies sealed. One thing to watch for is Hot filling turns pastry greasy and hard to seal.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 55 min🍽 8 servings🔥 335 cal
DinnerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup beef stock
  • 2 pie crust sheets
  • 1 egg
  • salt, pepper and thyme

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Cook onion and beef until browned, then stir in tomato paste, thyme and stock and reduce until thick.
  2. Step 2.Cool the filling so it will not melt the pastry before baking.
  3. Step 3.Cut pastry rounds, spoon filling onto half, and cut simple jack-o-lantern faces into the matching top rounds.
  4. Step 4.Seal edges with egg wash and crimp firmly, leaving the facial cutouts open as steam vents.
  5. Step 5.Bake at 200°C until golden and rest five minutes before serving.

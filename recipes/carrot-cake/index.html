Carrot Cake

A warmly spiced two-layer carrot cake with finely grated carrot, toasted walnuts, and a cool cream cheese finish.

KITCHEN NOTE

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 cup neutral vegetable oil
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups finely grated carrots
  • 1 cup toasted chopped walnuts
  • 16 ounces cream cheese frosting

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F. Prepare two 9-inch cake pans with parchment rounds, grease, and a light dusting of flour.
  2. Step 2.Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, and salt until the spices and leaveners are distributed evenly.
  3. Step 3.In a large bowl, whisk the oil, both sugars, eggs, and vanilla until the mixture is glossy and no longer grainy.
  4. Step 4.Fold the dry mixture into the liquid mixture only until the flour disappears; avoid working the batter longer than necessary.
  5. Step 5.Fold through the grated carrots and walnuts, breaking apart any clumps of carrot so they are spread through both layers.
  6. Step 6.Divide the batter evenly between the pans. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the tops spring back and a tester carries a few damp crumbs.
  7. Step 7.Leave the layers in their pans for 15 minutes, then turn them onto a rack and cool completely.
  8. Step 8.Sandwich the layers with cream cheese frosting, coat the top and sides, and chill briefly before cutting.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Finely grate whole carrots shortly before mixing for the most even crumb.
  • Toasting the walnuts first gives them a cleaner, nuttier flavour.
  • Wait until both layers are completely cool before adding the frosting.

! Common Mistakes

  • Packaged shredded carrot can be too thick and dry for a tender cake.
  • Too much mixing after flour is added can make the layers heavy.
  • Frosting warm cake layers causes the cream cheese coating to soften and slide.

↔ Substitutions

  • Pecans work in place of walnuts.
  • Add raisins if their chewy sweetness is wanted.
  • Replace part of the oil with applesauce for a lighter-style cake, noting that the crumb will be less rich.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate the covered frosted cake for up to 5 days.
  • Let a refrigerated slice stand briefly before eating for a softer texture.
  • Freeze unfrosted wrapped layers for up to 2 months.

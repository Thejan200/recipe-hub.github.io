Christmas Spritz Cookies

Buttery pressed cookies with crisp edges and a tender middle, made for festive shapes, sprinkles and a generous holiday cookie tin.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Use room-temperature butter, not melted butter. Keep this in mind too: Greasing the tray prevents the dough from releasing from the press.

Published 2026-11-11

⏱ 34 min🍽 36 servings🔥 95 cal
DessertUSAChristmas
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon almond extract
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • Red and green sanding sugar

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F and leave your baking sheets ungreased; the dough needs a bare surface to release cleanly from a cookie press.
  2. Step 2.Cream butter and sugar until pale and fluffy, then beat in the egg, vanilla and almond extract until the bowl smells sweet and nutty.
  3. Step 3.Mix in flour and salt just until no dry streaks remain; the dough should be soft enough to press but not greasy or sticky.
  4. Step 4.Load the press, hold it flat against the tray and make one decisive click for each cookie, lifting straight up so the shape stays tidy.
  5. Step 5.Decorate with sanding sugar before baking, then bake one tray at a time until the edges are set but still pale, about 7 to 9 minutes.
  6. Step 6.Cool on the tray for five minutes before moving the delicate cookies to a rack, then store only after they are completely cold.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use room-temperature butter, not melted butter.

! Common Mistakes

  • Greasing the tray prevents the dough from releasing from the press.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use lemon extract instead of almond for a brighter cookie.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Store airtight at room temperature for up to 5 days.

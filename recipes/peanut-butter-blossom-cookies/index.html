Peanut Butter Blossom Cookies

Soft, salty-sweet peanut butter cookies finished with a chocolate kiss while they are still warm—the kind of tray that disappears at a holiday cookie swap.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Use a room-temperature egg so the dough comes together evenly. To avoid a common problem, remember: Pressing the kisses in immediately can make the cookies spread too much.

Published 2026-11-11

⏱ 30 min🍽 30 servings🔥 145 cal
DessertUSAChristmas

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup light brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • 30 chocolate kiss candies, unwrapped

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F and line two baking sheets with parchment; unwrapping the chocolate kisses first makes the final step calm rather than hurried.
  2. Step 2.Beat the butter, peanut butter, granulated sugar and brown sugar until creamy and light, scraping the bowl once so no dry sugar is hiding at the bottom.
  3. Step 3.Beat in the egg and vanilla, then fold in flour, baking soda and salt only until a soft dough forms; overmixing makes these cookies less tender.
  4. Step 4.Roll level tablespoons of dough into smooth balls, place them well apart on the trays and bake until puffed with lightly set edges, about 9 to 11 minutes.
  5. Step 5.Let the cookies sit on the hot tray for two minutes, then gently press one chocolate kiss into the centre of every cookie so the base softens without melting away.
  6. Step 6.Cool completely before stacking; the chocolate needs time to set, and a short rest keeps each blossom neat enough for gifting.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use a room-temperature egg so the dough comes together evenly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Pressing the kisses in immediately can make the cookies spread too much.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use milk chocolate stars in place of kisses if preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Keep airtight at room temperature for up to 4 days.

Taco Soup

A hearty one-pot taco soup with browned beef, two kinds of beans, corn, tomatoes, and build-your-own Tex-Mex toppings.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Rinsing canned beans helps keep the soup cleaner-tasting and reduces some surface sodium. One thing to watch for is Too much beef fat left in the pot can make the broth feel greasy.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (10 oz) tomatoes with green chiles
  • 3 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) pinto beans, rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Shredded cheese, sour cream, avocado, and tortilla chips, for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Warm the oil in a large soup pot over medium-high heat, then cook the ground beef and onion together, breaking the beef into small pieces as it browns.
  2. Step 2.If there is a large amount of rendered fat, drain it carefully. Stir in garlic and taco seasoning until the mixture smells fragrant.
  3. Step 3.Add diced tomatoes, tomatoes with green chiles, and beef broth, scraping the bottom of the pot to lift the browned flavour into the soup.
  4. Step 4.Mix in black beans, pinto beans, corn, and salt, then bring the pot to a light boil.
  5. Step 5.Lower to a steady simmer and cook uncovered for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally as the broth becomes slightly more substantial.
  6. Step 6.Confirm the beef is cooked and the beans are hot, then taste before adding any more salt because seasoning levels vary between canned ingredients.
  7. Step 7.Let the pot stand off the heat for 5 minutes to settle before serving.
  8. Step 8.Serve with cheese, sour cream, avocado, and tortilla chips on the side so each bowl can be finished to taste.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Rinsing canned beans helps keep the soup cleaner-tasting and reduces some surface sodium.
  • Give the beef time to brown before adding broth for a more savoury base.
  • A topping bar keeps chips crunchy and makes the soup easy to adapt for different diners.

! Common Mistakes

  • Too much beef fat left in the pot can make the broth feel greasy.
  • Adding salt before tasting can oversalt a soup already seasoned by taco mix and canned tomatoes.
  • Adding avocado, dairy, or chips while the soup cooks removes the contrast those toppings provide.

↔ Substitutions

  • Ground turkey is a leaner swap for ground beef.
  • Kidney beans can replace pinto beans without changing the method.
  • Add finely chopped jalapeño with the onion for additional heat.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool and refrigerate the plain soup in a covered container for up to 4 days.
  • Keep avocado, dairy toppings, and tortilla chips separate until serving.
  • Freeze the soup base without toppings for up to 3 months.

Glazed Christmas Ham

A brown-sugar, orange, and clove glazed spiral ham with caramelized edges and tender slices for a festive holiday table.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Keep the first part of baking covered so the spiral slices stay juicy. Keep this in mind too: Glazing too early can make the sugar burn before the ham is hot.

Ingredients

  • 1 fully cooked bone-in spiral ham, 8 to 10 pounds
  • 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Orange slices, for garnish

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 325°F and place the ham cut-side down in a roasting pan with a small splash of water; cover tightly with foil.
  2. Step 2.Warm brown sugar, orange juice, Dijon mustard, vinegar, cloves and pepper in a saucepan until the sugar dissolves and the glaze is smooth.
  3. Step 3.Bake the covered ham until warmed through, then remove the foil and brush a generous layer of glaze between the spiral slices and across the surface.
  4. Step 4.Return the ham to the oven uncovered, brushing with more glaze every 15 minutes until the edges caramelize and the centre reaches 140°F.
  5. Step 5.Rest for 15 minutes so the juices settle, then spoon the pan glaze over the carved slices.
  6. Step 6.Serve with orange slices and any remaining warmed glaze alongside the platter.

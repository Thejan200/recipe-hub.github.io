Salmon Cakes

Crisp salmon cakes with herbs and a tender centre.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 25 min🍽 4 servings🔥 290 cal
SeafoodCanadaDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Natasha's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 14.75 ounces canned wild pink salmon, drained
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1/2 cup plain bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Drain the salmon well and remove any large bones or skin.
  2. Step 2.Combine salmon, eggs, breadcrumbs, mayonnaise, mustard, lemon juice and parsley in a bowl.
  3. Step 3.Season lightly, then mix just until the mixture holds together.
  4. Step 4.Shape into eight even patties and chill for 10 minutes.
  5. Step 5.Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  6. Step 6.Cook patties without crowding for 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  7. Step 7.Turn only when the underside is deeply golden.
  8. Step 8.Drain briefly and serve hot with lemon.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Chill the shaped patties briefly so they hold together when turned.
  • Drain canned salmon well and remove any large skin or bone pieces you do not want in the finished cakes.
  • Use medium heat so the crumb coating browns before the centre dries out.

! Common Mistakes

  • Turning the cakes before the first side has formed a golden crust.
  • Making the mixture too wet, which makes patties difficult to shape.
  • Crowding the pan, which lowers the heat and softens the coating.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use cooked flaked salmon in place of canned salmon, keeping the mixture fairly dry.
  • Use panko or fresh breadcrumbs for the coating according to the texture you prefer.
  • Serve with lemon wedges and a simple herb yogurt sauce if tartar sauce is not available.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooked salmon cakes in a covered container for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat in a skillet, oven, or air fryer until hot throughout and crisp outside.
  • For best texture, freeze shaped uncooked cakes separated on a tray, then cook from thawed.

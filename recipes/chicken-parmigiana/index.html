Chicken Parmigiana

Australian pub-style chicken parmigiana with crisp crumbed chicken, tomato sauce, ham and bubbling cheese.

Published 2026-10-21

⏱ 45 min🍽 4 servings🔥 690 cal

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breast cutlets
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan
  • olive oil, for frying
  • 1 cup tomato passata
  • 4 thin slices ham
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • chips and salad, to serve

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Place each chicken cutlet between baking paper and gently flatten it to an even thickness, seasoning both sides with salt and black pepper.
  2. Step 2.Set out three shallow bowls with flour, beaten eggs, and panko mixed with Parmesan, keeping the crumb mixture loose and evenly combined.
  3. Step 3.Coat one cutlet at a time in flour, then egg, then the breadcrumb mixture, pressing only enough for the crumbs to adhere well.
  4. Step 4.Heat a thin layer of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat and fry the crumbed chicken until both sides are deep golden.
  5. Step 5.Transfer the chicken to a lined tray and spoon tomato passata over the centre of each piece, leaving a little crisp edge exposed.
  6. Step 6.Lay ham over the sauce, scatter mozzarella on top, and grill until the cheese is melted, spotted and beginning to bubble.
  7. Step 7.Rest the finished parmigiana for two minutes so the hot topping settles and the crumb coating stays as crisp as possible.
  8. Step 8.Serve immediately with hot chips and a simple green salad, adding extra tomato sauce only at the table if desired.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use thin, even chicken cutlets so the crumb coating browns before the meat dries out.

! Common Mistakes

  • Covering the whole cutlet with sauce, which makes the crumb coating soggy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a mild provolone with the mozzarella if you prefer a more savoury cheese topping.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled leftovers for up to two days and reheat in an oven rather than a microwave.

German-Style Chicken Schnitzel

Thin chicken cutlets with a crisp, airy crumb, bright lemon, and a simple pan-fried finish inspired by German schnitzel dinners.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Use similarly sized cutlets so the chicken finishes at the same time. Keep this in mind too: Crowding the skillet drops the oil temperature and makes the crumb greasy.

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breast cutlets
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups fine dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/3 cup neutral oil
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Place the chicken between sheets of baking paper and gently pound it to an even thickness, keeping each cutlet wide rather than tearing the meat into thin spots.
  2. Step 2.Set out three shallow bowls with seasoned flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs mixed with paprika, salt, and pepper so the coating can be applied without rushing.
  3. Step 3.Coat one cutlet in flour and tap away the excess, then dip it through egg and let the extra drip back into the bowl before adding breadcrumbs.
  4. Step 4.Press the crumbs on lightly instead of packing them down; a looser layer lets the crust puff and stay delicate as the chicken cooks.
  5. Step 5.Heat oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat until a breadcrumb sizzles immediately, then lower the heat slightly so the coating browns before the chicken dries out.
  6. Step 6.Fry the cutlets in batches, turning once, until both sides are deeply golden and the thickest part reaches 165°F; add a little more oil between batches if needed.
  7. Step 7.Transfer each schnitzel to a rack or paper-lined plate and season with a small pinch of salt while it is hot, which keeps the crust crisp rather than steamy.
  8. Step 8.Serve straight away with lemon wedges and a simple potato or cucumber side, squeezing lemon over the surface only when it reaches the plate.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use similarly sized cutlets so the chicken finishes at the same time.

! Common Mistakes

  • Crowding the skillet drops the oil temperature and makes the crumb greasy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use turkey cutlets in place of chicken, cooking them to 165°F.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days and reheat on a rack in a hot oven.

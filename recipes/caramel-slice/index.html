Dessert · Medium

Caramel Slice

Australian caramel slice with a shortbread base, baked caramel middle and chocolate top.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 170 min🍽 16 servings🔥 310 cal
DessertAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Poles Patisserie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1/2 cup coconut
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 125 g butter
  • 395 g sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tablespoons golden syrup
  • 200 g milk chocolate

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Line a square tin and combine flour, coconut, brown sugar and melted butter for the crumbly base.
  2. Step 2.Press the mixture into the tin evenly and bake until lightly golden before adding the caramel layer.
  3. Step 3.Warm condensed milk, butter and golden syrup over low heat, stirring constantly until thick and glossy.
  4. Step 4.Pour caramel over the base and bake gently until the surface is set with a slight wobble.
  5. Step 5.Cool fully, then melt chocolate and spread it in a smooth, even layer across the caramel.
  6. Step 6.Chill until set, then cut with a warm knife into tidy squares before serving.

Sausage and Peppers

Italian-American pork sausages simmered with sweet bell peppers, onions, garlic, tomatoes, and herbs in a hearty skillet sauce.

⏱ 50 min🍽 6 servings🔥 470 cal

Ingredients

  • 6 Italian pork sausages, sweet or hot
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 cup crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat a large skillet over medium heat, add the olive oil and sausages, and brown them on several sides without trying to cook them completely through.
  2. Step 2.Transfer the browned sausages to a plate, leaving the flavorful drippings in the skillet.
  3. Step 3.Add the onion and all three bell peppers and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and develop a few browned edges.
  4. Step 4.Stir in the garlic, oregano, and basil and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant without allowing the garlic to scorch.
  5. Step 5.Add the crushed tomatoes and broth, scraping the bottom of the pan to dissolve the browned bits into the sauce.
  6. Step 6.Return the sausages and any accumulated juices to the skillet, nestling them among the peppers and onions.
  7. Step 7.Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover partially, and simmer for 12 to 15 minutes until the sausages are cooked through.
  8. Step 8.Confirm that pork sausages have reached 160°F internally, then uncover and simmer a few minutes if the sauce needs to thicken.
  9. Step 9.Taste the peppers and sauce before adding salt because sausage can contribute significant seasoning, then adjust pepper and herbs as needed.
  10. Step 10.Serve hot on crusty rolls, over polenta or pasta, or as a plated entrée with the peppers and sauce spooned over the sausages.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Brown sausages before simmering for deeper flavor.
  • Slice peppers evenly so they cook at the same rate.
  • Taste before adding salt because sausage is already seasoned.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling the sauce hard can split sausage casings.
  • Overcooking peppers makes them mushy.
  • Crowding a small skillet prevents browning.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a mix of sweet and hot Italian sausage.
  • Use fire-roasted diced tomatoes instead of crushed tomatoes.
  • Use vegetable broth if preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate sausages with peppers and sauce for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat gently in a covered skillet.
  • Freeze cooled portions for up to 2 months.

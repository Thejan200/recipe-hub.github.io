Sausage Casserole

An oven-baked British sausage casserole with tomato, stock, vegetables, herbs, and a rich, thick gravy.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 120 min🍽 4 servings🔥 981 cal
PorkUKDinnerChristmas

Video: Backyard Chef

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6–8 large pork sausages (about 700 g)
  • 1 large onion, sliced (150 g)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced (15 g)
  • 1 medium carrot, diced (100 g)
  • 1 celery stick, diced (50 g)
  • 3 tbsp steel-cut oats (45 g)
  • 800 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée (30 g)
  • 650 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (15 ml)
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (5 g)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (2 g)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (2 g)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Put the sausages in a casserole dish and cook for 20–25 minutes, turning once halfway through, until browned.
  2. Step 2.Add the sliced onion, garlic, carrot, celery, steel-cut oats, chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, beef stock, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, thyme, smoked paprika, black pepper, salt, and parsley to the dish.
  3. Step 3.Stir everything together well, cover the dish, and reduce the oven to 170°C fan (190°C conventional).
  4. Step 4.Bake covered for 1 hour, stirring once halfway through so the sauce thickens evenly and the vegetables cook through.
  5. Step 5.Remove the lid and bake for a further 20–25 minutes, until the gravy is thick and the sausages are glossy and cooked through.
  6. Step 6.Taste for salt, spoon into warm bowls, and serve immediately.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use steel-cut oats, not rolled oats, to help the sauce thicken during baking.
  • Turn the sausages halfway through the first bake so they brown evenly.
  • Bake uncovered at the end to concentrate the tomato gravy.

! Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the initial browning leaves the sausages pale and less flavorful.
  • Using rolled oats instead of steel-cut oats changes the casserole texture.
  • Leaving the lid on for the final bake prevents the sauce from thickening.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use good-quality pork sausages as shown in the tutorial.
  • Use beef stock for the intended rich gravy.
  • Use fresh parsley as the final herb.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 3 days.
  • Freeze in airtight portions for up to 2 months.
  • Reheat until the sausages are hot through.

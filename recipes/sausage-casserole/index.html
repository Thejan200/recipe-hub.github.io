Sausage Casserole

A British-style baked sausage casserole with vegetables, tomato, stock, and a slow-reduced savoury gravy.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Steel-cut oats thicken the gravy differently from rolled oats. One thing to watch for is Skipping the brown stage reduces flavour in both sausage and sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6–8 large pork sausages (about 700 g)
  • 1 large onion, sliced (150 g)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced (15 g)
  • 1 medium carrot, diced (100 g)
  • 1 celery stick, diced (50 g)
  • 3 tbsp steel-cut oats (45 g)
  • 800 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée (30 g)
  • 650 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (15 ml)
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (5 g)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (2 g)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (2 g)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Brown the sausages in a casserole dish, turning once so both sides gain colour.
  2. Step 2.Add onion, garlic, carrot, celery, steel-cut oats, tomatoes, tomato purée, stock, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, thyme, smoked paprika, pepper, salt, and parsley.
  3. Step 3.Stir well, cover, and lower the oven to 170°C fan (190°C conventional).
  4. Step 4.Bake until the vegetables are tender and the sauce has started to thicken, stirring once midway so the oats and tomato are evenly distributed.
  5. Step 5.Remove the lid and bake until the gravy looks concentrated and the sausages are completely cooked.
  6. Step 6.Taste before serving and add salt only if needed.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Steel-cut oats thicken the gravy differently from rolled oats.
  • Turn the sausages once during browning for even colour.
  • The uncovered finish is what gives the tomato gravy a concentrated texture.

! Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the brown stage reduces flavour in both sausage and sauce.
  • Rolled oats create a different casserole texture.
  • Leaving the lid on to the end prevents excess liquid from reducing.

↔ Substitutions

  • Choose good-quality pork sausages with a flavour you enjoy.
  • Beef stock gives the sauce a deeper savoury profile.
  • Use fresh parsley as the final herb.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled leftovers for up to three days.
  • Freeze sealed portions for up to two months.
  • Reheat until the sausage centres are hot through.

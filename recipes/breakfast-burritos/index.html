Breakfast Burritos

Pan-crisped breakfast burritos filled with softly scrambled eggs, ham, melting cheese, and tomato for a hearty portable breakfast.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Keep the eggs a little soft because they finish cooking during the crisping stage. To avoid a common problem, remember: Cold tortillas are more likely to tear as they are rolled.

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 oz diced ham
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack or mozzarella
  • 1 small tomato, diced
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/8 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Warm the tortillas briefly so they bend easily without splitting.
  2. Step 2.Whisk eggs with sour cream, salt, and pepper until evenly combined.
  3. Step 3.Melt butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat and add the egg mixture.
  4. Step 4.Draw the eggs gently into soft curds, taking them off the heat while they still look moist.
  5. Step 5.Arrange tortillas flat and portion the eggs across the lower third of each one.
  6. Step 6.Add ham, cheese, and tomato, leaving enough empty tortilla at the sides and edge for a secure roll.
  7. Step 7.Fold the sides inward, then roll from the bottom into a compact burrito.
  8. Step 8.Warm a thin coating of oil in a clean skillet and put the burritos in seam-side down.
  9. Step 9.Turn until the exterior is crisp and golden and the cheese has melted inside.
  10. Step 10.Let the burritos rest briefly before slicing or wrapping for later.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep the eggs a little soft because they finish cooking during the crisping stage.
  • A moderate amount of filling makes rolling far easier.
  • Beginning seam-side down helps close the wrap.

! Common Mistakes

  • Cold tortillas are more likely to tear as they are rolled.
  • Wet fillings can soften the tortilla quickly.
  • Eggs cooked until dry may become rubbery after reheating.

↔ Substitutions

  • Cooked bacon or breakfast sausage can replace ham.
  • Cheddar works in place of Monterey Jack.
  • Leave out tomato when preparing burritos for freezing.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate wrapped burritos for up to three days.
  • Freeze individual burritos for up to one month.
  • Reheat until the centre is steaming hot.

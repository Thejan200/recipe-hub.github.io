Finger-Shaped Cheese Sticks

Crisp cheese pastry sticks with almond nails and a herby finish, made for a playful Halloween snack board.

KITCHEN NOTE

Practical cooking note: Keep the pastry cold until it goes into the oven for the best layers. One thing to watch for is Overfilling the strips lets cheese leak and burn on the tray.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 36 min🍽 6 servings🔥 260 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 18 blanched almonds
  • marinara sauce for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and line a large tray with baking paper, leaving room between each stick so the pastry can puff evenly.
  2. Step 2.Unroll the pastry on a lightly floured surface and scatter mozzarella, Parmesan and Italian seasoning over one half without piling cheese at the edges.
  3. Step 3.Fold the bare half over the cheese, press gently to seal, and roll once with a pin so the filling is held in a thin, even layer.
  4. Step 4.Cut the pastry into narrow strips, then lightly pinch one end of each strip to create a rounded fingertip shape rather than a blunt rectangle.
  5. Step 5.Brush every strip with beaten egg and press one almond at the rounded end, leaving the rest of the pastry visible as the edible finger nail.
  6. Step 6.Score a few shallow lines across each strip with the back of a knife; do not cut through the pastry, because the stick needs to stay intact.
  7. Step 7.Bake until the sticks are puffed, browned and the cheese has melted into the layers, rotating the tray once if one side colours faster.
  8. Step 8.Cool for five minutes so the cheese settles, then serve warm with marinara in a separate bowl for dipping.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep the pastry cold until it goes into the oven for the best layers.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overfilling the strips lets cheese leak and burn on the tray.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a small slice of red pepper in place of an almond for a nut-free nail.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Store refrigerated for two days and re-crisp in a hot oven.

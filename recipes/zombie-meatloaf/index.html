Zombie Meatloaf

A tender glazed meatloaf with olive eyes and pepper hair, designed as a cheerful Halloween centrepiece rather than a scary dish.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Use a thermometer instead of relying only on colour in the centre. A common setback is Skipping the rest makes the loaf shed juices when sliced.

⏱ 1 hr 15 min🍽 6 servings🔥 390 cal

Ingredients

  • 750 g lean ground beef
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 cup soft breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/3 cup barbecue sauce
  • olives and green bell pepper strips

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 180°C and line a loaf pan or small roasting dish so the cooked meatloaf can be lifted out without breaking its shape.
  2. Step 2.Combine the beef, onion, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce and garlic powder in a bowl, mixing only until no dry crumbs remain.
  3. Step 3.Press the mixture into a compact loaf with a gently rounded top; packing it too firmly makes the finished slices dense instead of tender.
  4. Step 4.Bake the loaf uncovered until it is nearly cooked through, then brush the surface with barbecue sauce to create a glossy, savoury glaze.
  5. Step 5.Return it to the oven until the centre reaches a safe cooked temperature, then let it rest so its juices redistribute before decorating.
  6. Step 6.Set olive halves into small cheese circles for eyes and tuck thin green-pepper strips across the top as playful zombie hair.
  7. Step 7.Arrange the decorations while the loaf is warm but not steaming hot so they stay visible instead of sliding into the glaze.
  8. Step 8.Slice with a sharp knife and serve with roasted vegetables, keeping the decorated end intact for a fun first presentation.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use a thermometer instead of relying only on colour in the centre.

! Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the rest makes the loaf shed juices when sliced.

↔ Substitutions

  • Ground turkey can replace beef, with an extra tablespoon of milk for tenderness.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate slices for up to three days and reheat until hot.

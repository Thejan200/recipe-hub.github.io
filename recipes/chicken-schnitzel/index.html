Chicken Schnitzel

Crisp golden chicken schnitzel with a lemon wedge and a fresh, easy side.

Published 2026-10-21

⏱ 30 min🍽 4 servings🔥 520 cal
ChickenAustraliaDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Kitchen Sanctuary

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breast cutlets
  • 1/2 cup plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • neutral oil, for frying
  • lemon wedges, to serve

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Place the chicken between sheets of baking paper and flatten the thickest parts until every cutlet cooks at the same pace.
  2. Step 2.Season the flour with salt and pepper, then mix the panko with garlic powder and paprika in a separate shallow bowl.
  3. Step 3.Dip each cutlet into flour, shake away the excess, coat in egg, then cover thoroughly with the seasoned breadcrumb mixture.
  4. Step 4.Let the crumbed chicken stand on a rack for five minutes so the coating hydrates and is less likely to separate in the pan.
  5. Step 5.Heat enough neutral oil to cover the base of a large skillet and cook the schnitzels in batches without overcrowding them.
  6. Step 6.Turn each schnitzel once it is deeply golden underneath, then cook the second side until the chicken is fully cooked through.
  7. Step 7.Drain the cooked schnitzels briefly on a rack rather than paper towels, which can trap steam under the crisp coating.
  8. Step 8.Serve hot with lemon wedges and your preferred salad or potatoes, squeezing lemon over only just before eating.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cook in batches to maintain a steady frying temperature.

! Common Mistakes

  • Turning the schnitzel repeatedly before the first side has formed a crust.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use fine dry breadcrumbs when panko is unavailable.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to two days and re-crisp in a hot oven.

German Schweinebraten

Slow-roasted pork with caraway, crackling, and a rich pan gravy for a classic German Sunday dinner.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Keep the skin dry during roasting so it can crackle properly. To avoid a common problem, remember: Pouring stock over the skin prevents it from becoming crisp.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless pork shoulder with skin
  • 2 teaspoons caraway seeds
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 carrots, chunked
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Pat the pork dry and score only the skin in close parallel lines, taking care not to cut deeply into the meat beneath the fat layer.
  2. Step 2.Rub the pork with crushed caraway, salt, and pepper, pressing seasoning into the cuts on the skin as well as over the meaty sides.
  3. Step 3.Set the onion and carrots in a roasting pan, place the pork skin-side up over them, and pour stock around the vegetables without wetting the skin.
  4. Step 4.Roast at a moderate temperature until the meat is tender, checking the pan once or twice and adding a small splash of water if the vegetables threaten to scorch.
  5. Step 5.Raise the oven heat near the end and watch closely as the skin blisters into crisp crackling; this final stage can move quickly.
  6. Step 6.Transfer the pork to a board to rest, then strain the pan juices into a saucepan and skim any excess surface fat if desired.
  7. Step 7.Whisk cornstarch with cold water, stir it into simmering juices, and cook just until the gravy coats a spoon without becoming gluey.
  8. Step 8.Slice the pork across the grain and serve with crackling, gravy, dumplings, and braised cabbage.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Keep the skin dry during roasting so it can crackle properly.

! Common Mistakes

  • Pouring stock over the skin prevents it from becoming crisp.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use boneless pork loin for leaner slices, though it will have less rich crackling.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate sliced pork and gravy separately for up to 3 days.

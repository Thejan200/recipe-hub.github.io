Baked Mummy Jalapeño Poppers

Cream-cheese jalapeño poppers wrapped in light pastry strips and baked until crisp.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Wear gloves when handling cut jalapeños. To avoid a common problem, remember: Overfilling causes the cheese to bubble out.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 40 min🍽 12 servings🔥 115 cal
AppetizerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 12 jalapeños
  • 225 g cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup cheddar
  • 1 sheet crescent dough
  • 12 olive slices

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C, halve the jalapeños and scrape out seeds and membranes with gloves if sensitive to chile oil.
  2. Step 2.Mix cream cheese and cheddar, then fill each pepper half without mounding above the edge.
  3. Step 3.Cut dough into thin strips and wind them over the filling, leaving a small face gap.
  4. Step 4.Bake until peppers soften and pastry is golden.
  5. Step 5.Add olive eyes after baking and cool five minutes before serving because the filling stays very hot.

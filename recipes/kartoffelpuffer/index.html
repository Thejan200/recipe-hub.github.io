German Kartoffelpuffer

Crisp German potato pancakes with tender centres, best served hot with applesauce or sour cream.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Squeeze the grated potato mixture very dry before adding egg and flour. To avoid a common problem, remember: Wet shreds create pale, soft pancakes instead of crisp ones.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 cup neutral oil
  • applesauce or sour cream for serving

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Peel the potatoes and grate them with the onion into a clean kitchen towel, working quickly so the mixture does not darken before frying.
  2. Step 2.Gather the towel and squeeze firmly over the sink to remove as much liquid as possible; dry potato shreds make a pancake that crisps instead of steaming.
  3. Step 3.Transfer the grated vegetables to a bowl and mix with eggs, flour, salt, and pepper until the shreds are lightly bound but not buried in batter.
  4. Step 4.Heat a thin layer of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until a small potato strand sizzles immediately when it touches the surface.
  5. Step 5.Spoon in small mounds and flatten them gently, leaving space between pancakes so their edges can turn crisp rather than merge together.
  6. Step 6.Cook until the underside is deep golden, flip once, and fry the second side until equally crisp and the centre is tender.
  7. Step 7.Move finished pancakes to a rack or paper-lined tray and keep them in a low oven while the remaining batter is cooked in batches.
  8. Step 8.Serve hot with applesauce for a sweet contrast or sour cream and chives for a savory plate.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Squeeze the grated potato mixture very dry before adding egg and flour.

! Common Mistakes

  • Wet shreds create pale, soft pancakes instead of crisp ones.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a food processor grater to shorten the prep time.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate for up to 2 days and re-crisp in an oven or air fryer.

Irish-style Stew

An Irish family lamb stew with carrots, celery, onion, and a layer of sliced potatoes baked until tender.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 120 min🍽 6 servings🔥 540 cal
DinnerUK
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: Jamie Oliver

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 1 kg lamb shoulder, trimmed and cut into chunks
  • 2 onions, roughly chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 4 large carrots, chopped
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 litre beef or lamb stock
  • 900 g potatoes, peeled and sliced
  • A knob of butter
  • Sea salt and ground black pepper to taste

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat one tablespoon of oil in a flameproof casserole. Brown the lamb in batches, then transfer to a plate.
  2. Step 2.Add the remaining oil, onions, celery and carrots to the pot. Cook until softened, about five minutes. Heat the oven to 160°C.
  3. Step 3.Return the lamb to the pot with the bay leaf and stock. Season gently and bring to a simmer.
  4. Step 4.Arrange the sliced potatoes over the stew and dot with butter. Cover and bake for about 90 minutes until the lamb is tender.
  5. Step 5.Uncover for another 10 minutes to color the potatoes. Remove the bay leaf and serve warm.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use shoulder for the best tender texture.
  • Skim the broth if needed for a cleaner finish.
  • Add parsley at the end for freshness.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling vigorously can toughen lamb and break up potatoes.
  • Thick potato slices may remain firm after the lamb is tender.
  • Skipping the uncovered finish leaves the potato top pale.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use beef if lamb is unavailable.
  • Use chicken stock instead of lamb stock.
  • Use pearl barley for a heartier stew.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cool promptly and refrigerate for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat until piping hot; freeze portions for up to 2 months.

Roast Beef

Simple roasted eye of round beef, sliced thinly for a Canadian family dinner.

Published 2026-09-27

⏱ 85 min🍽 6 servings🔥 484 cal
BeefCanadaDinner

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds beef eye of round roast
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Bring the roast toward room temperature for 45 minutes.
  2. Step 2.Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Step 3.Tie the roast at intervals if it is not already tied.
  4. Step 4.Mix salt, garlic powder and pepper.
  5. Step 5.Rub the seasoning evenly over all sides.
  6. Step 6.Place the roast on a rack in a roasting pan.
  7. Step 7.Roast until the centre reaches the chosen doneness temperature.
  8. Step 8.Rest loosely covered for 15 to 20 minutes, then slice thinly.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Let the roast sit at room temperature briefly only while you prepare the oven; do not leave it out for extended periods.
  • Use a thermometer in the centre of the thickest part instead of relying only on time.
  • Rest the roast before slicing so the juices redistribute and slices stay neater.

! Common Mistakes

  • Slicing immediately after roasting, which lets juices run out.
  • Cooking by clock alone despite differences in roast shape and oven accuracy.
  • Cutting with the grain instead of across it, which makes slices seem tougher.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use another lean roasting cut with a similar shape and adjust cooking time by temperature.
  • Use dried herbs if fresh herbs are unavailable, using less because dried herbs are more concentrated.
  • Serve with roasted root vegetables or a simple pan gravy made from the drippings.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled roast beef for up to 3 days.
  • Store slices with a little pan juice to help prevent drying.
  • Use chilled leftovers in sandwiches or reheat gently with sauce or broth.

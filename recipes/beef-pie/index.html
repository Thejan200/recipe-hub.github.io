Beef Pie

Golden puff-pastry beef pie with a rich savoury gravy filling.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Cool the filling before it touches the pastry. Keep this in mind too: Using a runny filling, which causes a soggy pie base.

Published 2026-10-21

⏱ 1 hr🍽 4 servings🔥 610 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tablespoon cornflour
  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook the onion slowly until it is softened, sweet and lightly golden around the edges.
  2. Step 2.Add the beef mince and break it into small pieces, cooking until no pink patches remain and excess moisture has evaporated.
  3. Step 3.Stir in tomato paste and Worcestershire sauce, letting the paste cook briefly so it develops a fuller savoury flavour.
  4. Step 4.Whisk cornflour into the beef stock, pour it into the skillet, and simmer until the filling is thick enough to hold its shape.
  5. Step 5.Cool the beef filling completely so the pastry stays flaky rather than steaming and becoming soft before it reaches the oven.
  6. Step 6.Line four individual pie dishes with pastry, divide in the cooled filling, and place pastry lids over the top, sealing the edges.
  7. Step 7.Brush the pastry with beaten egg and cut a small steam vent in each lid so the rich filling can bubble safely while baking.
  8. Step 8.Bake until the pastry is deeply golden and crisp, then rest the pies for several minutes before serving with peas or mash.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Cool the filling before it touches the pastry.

! Common Mistakes

  • Using a runny filling, which causes a soggy pie base.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use shortcrust pastry for the base if you prefer a sturdier pie.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled pies for up to two days and reheat in an oven.

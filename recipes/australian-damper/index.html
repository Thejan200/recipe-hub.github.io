Australian Damper

Simple rustic Australian damper bread with a crisp crust and soft centre.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 40 min🍽 8 servings🔥 175 cal
BreakfastAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: OnePotChefShow

Ingredients

  • 3 cups self-raising flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 40 g cold butter
  • 1 cup milk
  • extra flour for shaping

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 200°C and line a baking tray, keeping ingredients cool for a tender crumb.
  2. Step 2.Rub cold butter into self-raising flour and salt until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
  3. Step 3.Pour in milk gradually and bring the dough together with a knife without overworking it.
  4. Step 4.Turn onto a floured surface, pat into a round loaf and score a deep cross across the top.
  5. Step 5.Bake until golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath, covering loosely if it browns too fast.
  6. Step 6.Rest for ten minutes, slice while warm and serve with butter, jam or soup.

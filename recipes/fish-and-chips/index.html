Fish and Chips

Golden white-fish fillets in a light cold batter, paired with twice-cooked chips, peas, lemon and tartar sauce.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Use cold liquid and make the batter at the last minute for a lighter crust. To avoid a common problem, remember: Warm or overmixed batter can become heavy and oily.

Ingredients

  • 4 cod or haddock fillets, about 170 g each
  • 900 g potatoes, cut into chips
  • 150 g plain flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 50 g cornflour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 250 ml cold beer or sparkling water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 litre vegetable oil for frying
  • 200 g peas
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 4 tbsp tartar sauce

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Rinse the cut potatoes, dry them well, and fry them in batches at a moderate temperature until tender but pale. Drain them on a rack or paper-lined tray while the oil heats further.
  2. Step 2.Whisk the flour, cornflour, baking powder, salt and pepper together. Pour in the cold beer or sparkling water just before frying and whisk only until the batter comes together; a few small lumps are fine.
  3. Step 3.Pat the fish completely dry and dust it lightly with flour. This dry layer gives the batter something to cling to.
  4. Step 4.Raise the frying oil to a hotter temperature. Dip one fillet at a time into the batter, let the excess drip away, and lower it gently into the oil away from you.
  5. Step 5.Fry the fish until the coating is crisp and the thickest part flakes easily with a fork. Drain on a rack and keep it uncovered so steam does not soften the crust.
  6. Step 6.Return the chips to the hot oil for a second fry until deeply golden and crisp. Cook the peas while the final batch of chips drains.
  7. Step 7.Season the chips, plate the fish with peas, lemon wedges and tartar sauce, and serve immediately.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use cold liquid and make the batter at the last minute for a lighter crust.
  • Drying the fish well is more important than adding extra flour to the batter.
  • Use a rack after frying so steam can escape from all sides.

! Common Mistakes

  • Warm or overmixed batter can become heavy and oily.
  • Wet fish causes the coating to slide off in the fryer.
  • Adding too much food at once drops the oil temperature and softens the crust.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use sparkling water when you do not want to use beer.
  • Choose haddock, pollock, or another firm white fish in place of cod.
  • Serve crushed peas with mint in place of whole peas.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Fish and chips are at their best straight from the fryer.
  • Reheat leftovers uncovered in a hot oven until hot through and crisp.
  • Never seal hot fried food in an airtight container because trapped steam softens it.

Lamington Cake

A celebration-size lamington cake layered with chocolate, coconut, jam and cream.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 1 hr 30 min🍽 12 servings🔥 355 cal
DessertAustralia
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: SimpleCookingChannel

Ingredients

  • two vanilla sponge layers
  • 1/2 cup raspberry jam
  • 1 cup thickened cream
  • 2 cups icing sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 2 cups desiccated coconut

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Level cooled sponge layers so the finished cake sits flat and is easy to slice cleanly.
  2. Step 2.Spread one layer with raspberry jam and softly whipped cream, leaving a small border around the edge.
  3. Step 3.Place the second sponge layer on top and chill briefly so the filling becomes stable.
  4. Step 4.Whisk cocoa, icing sugar and milk into a spreadable chocolate icing with no dry lumps.
  5. Step 5.Cover the top and sides of the cake with icing, then press coconut over every exposed surface.
  6. Step 6.Chill until set, then slice with a warm knife and serve with extra berries if desired.

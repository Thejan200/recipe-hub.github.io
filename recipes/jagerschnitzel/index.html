Jägerschnitzel with Mushroom Gravy

Crisp pork cutlets finished with a savory mushroom gravy for a hearty German-style dinner.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Brown mushrooms in an uncrowded pan for a deeper savory flavor. Keep this in mind too: Adding gravy too early softens the schnitzel crust.

Published 2026-11-06

Ingredients

  • 4 thin pork cutlets
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 8 ounces cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 1 1/2 cups beef stock
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Pound the pork cutlets gently to an even thinness, then season them on both sides so they cook quickly and remain tender under the sauce.
  2. Step 2.Set up flour, egg, and breadcrumb bowls, coat the pork lightly, and place the breaded cutlets on a plate while the skillet and gravy ingredients are prepared.
  3. Step 3.Brown the mushrooms in butter over medium-high heat without stirring constantly, allowing their moisture to cook away before the onion is added.
  4. Step 4.Stir in onion and cook until soft, then sprinkle flour over the mixture and stir for one minute to remove the raw flour taste.
  5. Step 5.Slowly add stock while scraping the pan, whisk in mustard, and simmer until the gravy coats a spoon; season carefully because stock salt levels vary.
  6. Step 6.Keep the gravy warm over low heat while heating oil in a second skillet, then fry the breaded cutlets until golden on both sides and cooked through.
  7. Step 7.Set the schnitzel on warmed plates and spoon gravy across only part of each cutlet, leaving some crisp coating visible for contrast.
  8. Step 8.Serve promptly with spaetzle, potatoes, or green beans, and pass extra mushroom gravy at the table rather than soaking the crust in advance.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Brown mushrooms in an uncrowded pan for a deeper savory flavor.

! Common Mistakes

  • Adding gravy too early softens the schnitzel crust.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use button mushrooms or a mixture of mushrooms if cremini are unavailable.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Store cutlets and gravy separately for up to 3 days, then reheat separately.

Chicken Korma

A mild British Indian chicken korma with coconut cream, yogurt, almonds, and warm spices.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 30 min🍽 4 servings🔥 590 cal

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp ghee or sunflower oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 525 g chicken breast, cubed
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp minced ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp mild chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 120 ml full-fat plain yogurt
  • 120 ml coconut cream
  • 2 tbsp ground almonds
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 60 ml double cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the ghee or oil in a wide pan. Soften the finely chopped onion for about five minutes.
  2. Step 2.Add the cubed chicken breast and cook, turning, until its exterior is opaque. Stir in garlic, ginger, spices, salt, pepper and tomato purée.
  3. Step 3.Cook the spice mixture briefly, then stir in yogurt, coconut cream, ground almonds and sugar. Simmer gently for six to eight minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Step 4.Stir in the double cream off the heat. Scatter fresh coriander over the curry and serve.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use gentle heat once yogurt and cream are added.
  • Ground almonds thicken the sauce and keep it mild.
  • Rest for a few minutes before serving with rice.

! Common Mistakes

  • Boiling the yogurt sauce hard can cause it to split.
  • Undercooking the chicken is unsafe; check it is cooked through.
  • Adding cream before reducing the heat can make the sauce grainy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use sunflower oil in place of ghee.
  • Omit mild chilli powder for a milder curry.
  • Use blanched almond flour in place of ground almonds.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate cooled curry for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat gently until the chicken is piping hot; do not repeatedly reheat.

Apple Pie

A classic double-crust apple pie with cinnamon-spiced apple slices, a golden flaky top, and a filling allowed to set before serving.

KITCHEN NOTE

Cook’s focus: Mixing tart and sweeter apples gives the filling more depth. Keep this in mind too: Pulling dough to fit the plate can cause it to shrink back while baking.

Ingredients

  • 2 disks chilled pie dough
  • 6 cups peeled sliced apples
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, diced
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water
  • Coarse sugar, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Set the oven to 425°F and place a rimmed baking sheet on a lower rack to preheat; it will catch any juices and support browning underneath.
  2. Step 2.Roll one chilled dough disk into a round for a 9-inch pie plate. Ease it into the plate without pulling the dough, then refrigerate it while making the filling.
  3. Step 3.Combine apples, granulated sugar, brown sugar, flour, lemon juice, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt until the slices are evenly coated.
  4. Step 4.Spoon the apples into the cold crust, making a gentle mound in the centre, and scatter the diced butter across the top.
  5. Step 5.Roll out the remaining dough and lay it over the filling, or arrange it as a lattice. Trim, seal, and crimp the edge securely.
  6. Step 6.Brush with egg wash, add coarse sugar if desired, and cut vents through a solid top crust.
  7. Step 7.Bake for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 375°F and continue for 40 to 45 minutes until the crust is richly browned and juices are bubbling through the vents.
  8. Step 8.Cool on a rack for at least 3 hours so the juices thicken enough for neat slices.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Mixing tart and sweeter apples gives the filling more depth.
  • Cold pastry is easier to handle and bakes into a flakier crust.
  • Look for active bubbling in the centre before deciding the pie is done.

! Common Mistakes

  • Pulling dough to fit the plate can cause it to shrink back while baking.
  • Removing the pie before the filling bubbles can leave the flour undercooked and the base pale.
  • Cutting a hot pie releases juices before they have time to set.

↔ Substitutions

  • Prepared pie dough is a practical shortcut.
  • Allspice can replace nutmeg for a different warm spice note.
  • Tapioca starch may replace flour when a clearer fruit filling is preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cover and keep at room temperature for up to 1 day.
  • Refrigerate remaining pie for up to 4 days.
  • Warm slices in a 325°F oven until the crust regains some crispness.

Steak Sandwich

A hearty Australian-style steak sandwich with grilled beef, onions, salad and beetroot.

KITCHEN NOTE

Before you begin: Rest the steak before slicing it thinly across the grain. To avoid a common problem, remember: Building the sandwich with very hot steak, which wilts every fresh ingredient.

Ingredients

  • 2 beef steaks
  • 4 slices sturdy bread
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 slices pickled beetroot
  • lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • salt and black pepper

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Bring the steaks toward room temperature for a short time, then season both sides well with salt and black pepper before cooking.
  2. Step 2.Heat a heavy skillet until hot and cook the sliced onion with a little butter until it is soft, sweet and lightly caramelised.
  3. Step 3.Remove the onions, add the steaks to the hot pan, and cook until browned outside while keeping the centre at your preferred level of doneness.
  4. Step 4.Rest the steaks on a board for at least five minutes so their juices settle instead of running out as soon as they are sliced.
  5. Step 5.Toast the bread in the same pan or under a grill until crisp at the edges but still sturdy enough to hold the fillings.
  6. Step 6.Spread mayonnaise on the toast, then layer lettuce, tomato, pickled beetroot and the warm caramelised onions across the bottom slices.
  7. Step 7.Slice the rested steak thinly across the grain and arrange it evenly over the vegetables so every bite is easy to handle.
  8. Step 8.Close the sandwiches, press lightly, cut in half and serve immediately with chips or a simple salad on the side.

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Rest the steak before slicing it thinly across the grain.

! Common Mistakes

  • Building the sandwich with very hot steak, which wilts every fresh ingredient.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use a soft bread roll in place of sliced bread if preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • This sandwich is best assembled just before serving.

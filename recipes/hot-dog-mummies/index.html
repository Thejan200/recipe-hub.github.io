Hot Dog Mummies

Tender hot dogs wrapped in golden pastry strips with tiny mustard eyes for a quick Halloween tray.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Leave small gaps so the dough looks like bandages. A common setback is Wrapping too tightly stops the pastry from puffing.

Published 2026-10-29

⏱ 30 min🍽 8 servings🔥 220 cal
AppetizerUSAScary Halloween Recipes
↓ Jump to recipe

Ingredients

  • 8 hot dogs
  • 1 sheet crescent dough
  • 1 egg
  • mustard
  • ketchup

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 190°C and line a baking tray.
  2. Step 2.Cut dough into thin uneven strips to create a loose wrapped appearance.
  3. Step 3.Wind strips around each hot dog, leaving a small open space near one end for the face.
  4. Step 4.Brush the dough lightly with beaten egg and bake until the strips are puffed and golden.
  5. Step 5.Add two mustard dots after baking and serve warm with ketchup.

Chicken Tray Bake

Moroccan-style Australian chicken tray bake with roasted vegetables, chickpeas and warming spices.

Published 2026-10-24

⏱ 60 min🍽 4 servings🔥 590 cal
ChickenAustraliaDinner
↓ Jump to recipe

Watch the tutorial

Video: RecipeTin Eats

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 400 g chickpeas
  • 1 lemon
  • fresh coriander

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 200°C and toss chicken thighs with olive oil, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper.
  2. Step 2.Spread onion, carrots and drained chickpeas across a large roasting tray in an even layer.
  3. Step 3.Nestle seasoned chicken among the vegetables, skin-side up, so the skin can brown and crisp.
  4. Step 4.Roast until chicken is cooked through and vegetables are tender, turning vegetables once if needed.
  5. Step 5.Squeeze lemon over the hot tray and let it stand for five minutes so the juices settle.
  6. Step 6.Scatter coriander over the chicken and serve directly from the tray with couscous or salad.

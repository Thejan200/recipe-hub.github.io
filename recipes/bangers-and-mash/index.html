Bangers and Mash

Classic bangers and mash with juicy browned sausages, buttery mashed potatoes, and glossy onion gravy.

Published 2026-09-21

⏱ 50 min🍽 4 servings🔥 690 cal

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 900 g Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 120 ml milk, warmed
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 240 ml beef or chicken broth
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Boil the cubed potatoes in salted water until tender. Warm the milk separately.
  2. Step 2.Heat the olive oil in a skillet and cook the sausages, turning regularly, until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a warm plate.
  3. Step 3.Cook the sliced onion in the same pan until soft and lightly browned. Stir in the flour for one minute.
  4. Step 4.Gradually stir in the broth and optional Worcestershire sauce. Simmer until the onion gravy thickens; season to taste.
  5. Step 5.Drain the potatoes and mash thoroughly with the warm milk and butter. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Step 6.Divide the mash between plates, add the sausages, and pour over the onion gravy. Finish with parsley.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Do not pierce the sausages while browning.
  • Mash potatoes while hot for the smoothest texture.
  • Whisk flour into the onions before adding stock.

! Common Mistakes

  • Turning sausages too often prevents browning.
  • Cold milk cools the mash and makes it stiff.
  • Adding broth too quickly can leave flour lumps in the gravy.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use beef dripping instead of oil for pub flavor.
  • Use Dijon mustard if English mustard is unavailable.
  • Use vegetarian sausages for a meat-free version.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate the cooked components separately for up to 2 days.
  • Reheat thoroughly, loosening the gravy with a splash of water if necessary.

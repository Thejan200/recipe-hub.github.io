Key Lime Pie

Tangy Key lime pie with a graham cracker crust, creamy citrus filling, and whipped cream.

KITCHEN NOTE

Kitchen note: Bottled Key lime juice is acceptable if real Key limes are unavailable. A common setback is Overbaking can make the filling grainy.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup Key lime juice
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Lime slices, for garnish

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F and mix graham crumbs, melted butter, and sugar until the crumbs feel evenly moistened like damp sand.
  2. Step 2.Press the crumb mixture firmly into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch pie plate, using a cup to compact the crust evenly.
  3. Step 3.Bake the crust for 8 minutes until fragrant and lightly darker, then cool it while keeping the oven on for the filling.
  4. Step 4.Whisk egg yolks until slightly thickened, then whisk in sweetened condensed milk, Key lime juice, and lime zest until smooth.
  5. Step 5.Pour the filling into the warm crust and spread it level so the pie bakes evenly and slices cleanly after chilling.
  6. Step 6.Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the filling is set around the edges and just barely trembles in the center.
  7. Step 7.Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours so the citrus custard becomes cold and sliceable.
  8. Step 8.Whip cream with powdered sugar, dollop or pipe it on the chilled pie, and garnish with lime slices immediately before serving.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Bottled Key lime juice is acceptable if real Key limes are unavailable.
  • Compact the crumb crust firmly to prevent crumbling.
  • Chill fully for the cleanest slices.

! Common Mistakes

  • Overbaking can make the filling grainy.
  • Loose crumbs make the crust fall apart.
  • Adding whipped cream before chilling can make it weep.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use regular lime juice for a less floral but still tangy pie.
  • Use digestive biscuits instead of graham crackers.
  • Use meringue instead of whipped cream if preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate covered for up to 3 days.
  • Keep whipped cream loosely covered.
  • Freeze without whipped cream for up to 1 month.

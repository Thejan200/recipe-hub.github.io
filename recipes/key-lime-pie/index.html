Key Lime Pie

A chilled lime custard pie with a pressed crumb base, bright citrus flavour, and a light cream finish.

KITCHEN NOTE

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup Key lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon finely grated lime zest
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • Thin lime slices, optional

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 350°F. Combine the graham crumbs, butter, and sugar until every crumb has a light coating of butter.
  2. Step 2.Press the mixture across the base and up the side of a 9-inch pie plate. A flat-bottomed measuring cup helps make the thickness even.
  3. Step 3.Bake the crust for about 8 minutes, until it smells toasted and looks slightly deeper in colour. Let it stand while the filling is mixed.
  4. Step 4.Whisk the yolks in a bowl, then add the sweetened condensed milk, lime juice, and zest. Continue only until the mixture is smooth and fully combined.
  5. Step 5.Pour the custard into the prepared shell and level the surface with a gentle tilt of the dish.
  6. Step 6.Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, stopping when the outer edge is set and the centre has only a small tremor when moved.
  7. Step 7.Cool the pie at room temperature, then refrigerate it for at least 4 hours so the filling becomes firm enough for tidy portions.
  8. Step 8.Shortly before serving, whip the cream with powdered sugar and spoon or pipe it over the cold pie. Add lime slices if desired.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Use bottled Key lime juice when fresh Key limes are unavailable; choose a product made from real juice.
  • Press the crumb mixture firmly, especially where the base meets the side, to help the slices hold together.
  • Keep the pie chilled until the moment it is served for the best texture.

! Common Mistakes

  • Leaving the filling in the oven until it is entirely motionless can make the custard feel less smooth.
  • A lightly packed crust is likely to crumble when a slice is lifted.
  • Decorating with cream before the pie is cold can make the topping soften or slide.

↔ Substitutions

  • Regular Persian lime juice makes a good alternative, with a sharper citrus note.
  • Digestive biscuit crumbs can stand in for graham crumbs.
  • Top the finished pie with meringue instead of cream if that style is preferred.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Cover and refrigerate leftover pie for up to 3 days.
  • Keep cream-topped portions chilled and loosely protected from absorbing refrigerator odours.
  • For freezing, leave off the whipped cream and freeze the wrapped pie for up to 1 month; thaw it in the refrigerator.

Pork Tenderloin

Juicy oven-roasted pork tenderloin with a savory herb-and-garlic crust, seared first for deep flavor and a tender center.

⏱ 40 min🍽 4 servings🔥 330 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb pork tenderloin, silver skin removed
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tbsp avocado or olive oil
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, lightly crushed

Instructions

Follow each step in order. The directions below are written so a beginner can understand what to do, what to look for, and when to move on to the next step.

  1. Step 1.Heat the oven to 400°F and remove the silver skin from the tenderloin with a sharp knife, keeping as much lean meat intact as possible.
  2. Step 2.Pat the pork dry, then mix the salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and coriander in a small bowl.
  3. Step 3.Rub the tenderloin with one tablespoon of oil and press the seasoning blend over every side until the surface is evenly coated.
  4. Step 4.Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat, add the remaining oil, and wait until it shimmers before adding the pork.
  5. Step 5.Sear the tenderloin for about 2 minutes per side, rotating until the exterior is browned all around rather than pale or steamed.
  6. Step 6.Add the butter and crushed garlic to the skillet, briefly spoon the foaming butter over the pork, then transfer the pan to the oven.
  7. Step 7.Roast for about 12 to 16 minutes, checking the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer rather than cutting into the meat.
  8. Step 8.Remove the pork when the center reaches 145°F, transfer it to a cutting board, and tent loosely with foil.
  9. Step 9.Rest for 8 to 10 minutes so the juices redistribute and the internal temperature stabilizes before slicing.
  10. Step 10.Slice across the grain into medallions and spoon any resting juices over the meat before serving.

HELPFUL NOTES

Tips, Substitutions & Storage

Extra guidance to help you get a reliable result, avoid common problems, and know what to do with leftovers.

✓ Recipe Tips

  • Remove silver skin because it stays tough after cooking.
  • Use a thermometer to avoid overcooking this lean cut.
  • Rest before slicing to retain juices.

! Common Mistakes

  • Confusing pork loin with pork tenderloin changes the cooking time.
  • Skipping the sear reduces crust and flavor.
  • Cooking far beyond 145°F can dry the meat.

↔ Substitutions

  • Use dried rosemary and thyme instead of Italian seasoning.
  • Use neutral vegetable oil instead of avocado oil.
  • Add Dijon mustard beneath the seasoning for a tangy crust.

⌂ Storage & Reheating

  • Refrigerate sliced pork for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat gently to avoid drying out the lean meat.
  • Freeze cooled slices for up to 2 months.

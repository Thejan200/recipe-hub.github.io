Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What BiteSparks uses now

BiteSparks currently uses browser local storage for optional convenience features such as saved recipes, theme preferences, and remembering a cookie-notice choice.

Recipe ratings and security

If you voluntarily submit a recipe rating, BiteSparks stores a temporary anonymous rating session in your browser so that one current rating can be associated with that browser for that recipe. Supabase processes the rating data, and Cloudflare Turnstile may use cookies or similar technology to run the security check that protects ratings from automated submissions.

Google Analytics

With your consent, BiteSparks loads Google Analytics 4. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies to measure aggregated website use, including pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, and interactions. It is not loaded when you decline.

You can change your choice by clearing BiteSparks site data in your browser and choosing again when the notice appears. You can also use Google’s browser controls and privacy tools to manage cookies.

Advertising

Google AdSense is not currently active on BiteSparks and no Google ads are currently served by the site. If advertising is enabled in the future, BiteSparks will update this policy and provide the appropriate consent choices before activation.

Third-party content

Embedded YouTube videos and external links are controlled by their providers. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies when you interact with their content.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. If non-essential technologies are introduced, the site will provide choices relevant to those technologies before they are enabled.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

What we use now

Recipe Hub currently uses browser local storage for optional convenience features such as saved recipes and remembering the cookie-consent choice. This is not the same as a server-side account database.

Future analytics and advertising

Analytics, advertising, and other non-essential tracking may be added later. Those services should not be enabled until the relevant provider configuration, privacy disclosures, and consent mechanism are in place.

Your choices

You can decline non-essential cookies when the consent banner is presented. You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Essential functionality may be affected when browser storage is disabled.

Third-party content

Embedded third-party content such as YouTube may set or access data according to the provider's own policies. Review the provider's privacy and cookie documentation for details.

Updates

This policy will be updated when the website's storage, analytics, advertising, or third-party integrations change.

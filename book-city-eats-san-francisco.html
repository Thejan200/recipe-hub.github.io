City Eats: San Francisco

50 Recipes from the Best of the City by the Bay

by Trevor Felch

Published2023
FormatHardcover
ISBN-101646434250

What this book is about

Trevor Felch’s cookbook celebrates San Francisco through 50 recipes tied to the city’s distinctive culinary identity. It highlights the multicultural influences and local dining culture that shape food in the Bay Area, making it part cookbook and part snapshot of a major American food city.

Cooking focus

A culinary tour of San Francisco through recipes connected to the city’s restaurants and multicultural food scene.

Air Fryer Cookbook

600 Effortless Air Fryer Recipes for Beginners and Advanced Users

by Jenson William

Published2019
FormatPaperback

What this book is about

Jenson William’s collection is designed as a broad reference for air-fryer owners, with hundreds of recipes across common meal categories. It suits readers who want a large volume of ideas and an entry point to using an air fryer for more than snacks or reheating.

Cooking focus

A large air-fryer recipe collection spanning meats, vegetables, snacks, sides, breakfasts, lunches, sweets, and desserts.

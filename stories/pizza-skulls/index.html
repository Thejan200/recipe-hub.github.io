SCARY HALLOWEEN RECIPES

Crisp pizza pockets in a skull shape

Pizza dough, sauce, cheese and a mould are all you need for a shareable spooky snack.

PREP THE PAN

Grease every small detail

Coat the eye sockets and edges of the skull mould well so the hot pockets release cleanly after baking.

BUILD THE BASE

Press dough into each cavity

Use dough slightly larger than the mould. Ease it into the contours without stretching it thin at the edges.

FILL LIGHTLY

A little filling gives a better shape

Add sauce, mozzarella, sausage and pepper below the rim. Overfilling is the main reason pockets split.

SEAL AND BAKE

Close the skulls before the oven

Top with dough, press the edges closed and bake at 190°C until firm and browned around the details.

SERVE SAFELY

Let the cheese cool briefly

Turn the skulls out carefully and serve with warm pizza sauce. The full recipe has the exact portions.

SCARY HALLOWEEN RECIPES

A golden pie with a playful monster face

A warm beef-and-vegetable pie becomes a Halloween centrepiece with a few simple pastry decorations.

THE FILLING

Keep the filling thick, not wet

Cook onion and carrot until soft, brown the beef, then simmer with tomato paste, thyme and stock until the mixture holds on a spoon.

THE CRUST

Cool first for a crisp base

Let the filling lose its steam before it meets the pastry. This helps the lower crust bake rather than turn soft.

THE FACE

Seal, vent and decorate

Crimp the pastry edge firmly. Add olive, cheese and pepper pieces for the face, leaving a few small vents clear.

THE BAKE

Bake until deeply golden

Bake at 200°C until the pastry is crisp and richly coloured. Rest the pie for ten minutes so the slices hold together.

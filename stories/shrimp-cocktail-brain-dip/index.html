Shrimp Cocktail Brain Dip

A chilled shrimp cocktail dip piped into a playful brain-like swirl for a Halloween seafood starter.

GET READY

Start with the key components

Gather g cream cheese, cup cocktail sauce, tablespoon horseradish and set up your workspace before you begin.

PREP

Build the Halloween detail with care

Blend cream cheese, half the cocktail sauce and horseradish until smooth enough to pipe.

COOK

Let texture lead the timing

Spoon the mixture into a piping bag with a round tip and pipe two winding brain-like lobes onto a chilled plate.

SERVE

Finish, cool, and share

Serve cold with crackers and keep refrigerated between servings.

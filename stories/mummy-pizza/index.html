SCARY HALLOWEEN RECIPES

A fast pizza dressed as a mummy

This easy personal pizza uses pita, tomato sauce, mozzarella strips and olive eyes for a fun dinner.

START CRISP

Heat the oven to 220°C

Place the pitas on a baking sheet so their bases can crisp while the cheese melts.

ADD A THIN LAYER

Keep the sauce light

Spread sauce over each pita, leaving a narrow border. A thin coating keeps the base from becoming soft.

MAKE THE BANDAGES

Leave gaps between cheese strips

Lay mozzarella in uneven bands so the red sauce remains visible. Save a small open space for olive eyes.

BAKE UNTIL BUBBLY

Look for crisp edges

Bake until the pita is firm and the mozzarella has melted and lightly browned at the edges.

HALLOWEEN DINNER

Slice after a short rest

Let the pizza cool just enough to set the cheese. The full recipe includes exact amounts and substitutions.

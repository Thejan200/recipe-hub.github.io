What this book is about

Tieghan Gerard’s second Half Baked Harvest cookbook is built around practical ways to get flavorful food on the table with less hands-on effort. The collection emphasizes flexible comfort food, make-ahead planning, and recipes designed for busy home cooks who still want bold, satisfying meals.

Cooking focus

Approachable comfort food with make-ahead, overnight, meal-prep, and streamlined cooking ideas.

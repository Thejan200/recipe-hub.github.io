What this book is about

Suzy Karadsheh’s cookbook draws on Mediterranean and Middle Eastern traditions while keeping the recipes accessible for modern home kitchens. The collection emphasizes colorful produce, herbs, grains, legumes, proteins, and bright seasonings in dishes intended to be practical enough for repeat cooking.

Cooking focus

Mediterranean and Middle Eastern inspired cooking with bold flavor, vegetables, grains, proteins, and weeknight-friendly recipes.

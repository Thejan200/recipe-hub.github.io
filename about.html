ABOUT BITESPARKS

Practical recipe guidance for everyday cooks.

BiteSparks is an independent online recipe publisher created and managed by M.G.Y. Thejan Mahakumbura in Sri Lanka.

What BiteSparks publishes

BiteSparks publishes recipe pages with ingredients, timings, step-by-step directions, practical notes, and where available, clearly credited third-party video references. The site is designed to help home cooks decide what to make and follow a recipe with more confidence.

Our editorial approach

Before publication, recipes are reviewed for readable instructions, sensible ingredient lists, clear timing information, basic food-safety context where relevant, and a useful structure for home cooks. Cooking results can still vary with ingredients, equipment, portions, altitude, and individual technique, so readers should use their judgment and follow local food-safety guidance.

Corrections and updates

Accuracy matters. If you spot an error, unclear step, broken link, or issue with a recipe, please contact BiteSparks with the recipe name and details. We review correction requests and update published information where appropriate.

Independence and transparency

BiteSparks is an online-only publication; it does not represent a restaurant, shop, or physical cooking school. Advertising is not currently active while the site’s Google AdSense application is under review. If advertising or affiliate relationships are enabled in the future, relevant disclosures will be updated before they are used.
